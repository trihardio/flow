# Trading tracker

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`. The calculators are in `notes/calculators.md`, forex in `notes/forex.md`: both are parts of this tracker.

- **Trading tracker** (`TradeTracker`, a module-level component above `PnlCalendar`; `market` is `"crypto"`, `"idx"` or `"fx"`): Rassam asked for everything to be the same as the finance side (yearly panel, monthly panel, all the GUIs) but crypto-PnL styled, after Bybit, Binance and Axiom, and then for the IDX/IHSG panel to be "the same thing" as the crypto one.
  - It is a component of its own on purpose: it reuses the finance side's styles, classes and animations (`S`, `.ycell`, `.cell`, `.flow-card`, the chart classes, the title flicker, the wrapped), but none of its state, so work on it can't break the finance panels. When a finance GUI changes, mirror the change here.
  - `MARKETS` lists everything that differs between the two: storage keys, the currency trades are kept in and its decimals, whether a trade has a side, the words (pair / stock, Coins / Stocks), the grey text after a name, the test data. Add a difference there, not as an `if` on the market name.
  - Data: `trades`, day key → `[{ id, pnl, pair, side }]`. `pnl` is the realised PnL, signed: US dollars with cents for crypto, whole rupiah for stocks. A break-even trade (the crypto journal's Breakeven) is one at exactly 0, or one marked `be: true` that carries the few dollars it closed at, plus or minus (5 October 2026: "make me able to input $ for breakeven"). Either counts as a trade, neither won nor lost (`resOf`, `tally`; the dollars still count in the sums), with a grey dot and "1BE" on its day. `side` is `"long"` or `"short"` (stocks are always long, and show no side). Crypto is stored under `pnl-crypto-usd`, stocks under `pnl-idx-idr`, never mixed with each other or with `pnl-calendar-idr`. `migrateTrades` sanitises what is loaded or imported. Each side has its own Export / Import; since 11 October 2026 a file of another side is not refused but sent to the side it belongs to (see Import).
  - **Day tiles are coloured by the day's net result only** (6 October 2026): above zero green, below red, exactly zero yellow (#e3cd77), with a faint wash of it on the tile (about 5%) and neutral charcoal at zero. A $0 day that held a win used to be green. The tile's hover says `W +…`, `L −…` and, when there is one, `BE …`.
  - **Chart hover compares** (`GlassTip`'s `sub`): on the analysis charts, the change on the day and the line of the period before at that day with the difference; on the chart pop-up's running line, what was made since the point before, and with Compare on the compared period's figure at the same spot and the difference. The finance pop-up has none yet.
  - On the % chart the line of the period before is thinner and at 38% so the current one leads.
  - What maps to what: gross profit ↔ income, gross loss ↔ spending, net PnL ↔ what's left.
  - **Sums of trades are rounded to the cent** (`tally`, `cryptoMonth`, `cryptoYear`; 9 October 2026, a bug hunt he asked for): added up as they are, cents drift (0.1 + 0.2 − 0.3 is not 0), and a day that came to nothing read as a green one. **A day on exactly zero is neither green nor red**: not in the Green days tile, and in the wrapped it neither adds to the streak nor breaks it.
  - **The bar is the boxes under it, by their net result** (`up` / `down` in `cryptoMonth` / `cryptoYear`, `grossBar`; 6 October 2026): on the month, what the green days made against what the red days lost; on the year, the green months against the red months.
