# Light mode

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Light mode** (13 October 2026: "make light mode version, can be toggled on and off in settings"). The apps (finance and the three trading sides, with their pop-ups and the sidebar) have a light look. **The panels before an app stay dark** (the opening screen, the login, the selections, wallets and trading panels): my choice, not his words; the liquid-glass pass left them alone for the same reason, they are a lit scene with a backdrop of their own. The look is the app's, not an account's (`THEME_KEY` = `flow-theme` in `localStorage`, nobody's data, in no backup), and it changes at once, in place: the page is never loaded again.
- **How it works** (the block "Light mode" near the top of `src/App.jsx`). The dark look is the one written down; the light one follows from it.
  - Every colour is written `light-dark(dark, light)`. The light one is worked out from the dark one by what it is used for (`toLight`, in OKLCH): text (light greys become ink, a colour is deepened until it reads on white at 4 to 1), the fill of a surface (dark fills become near-white, a white veil a faint dark one, a dim a weaker dim), a line, a drawing's fill or stroke, an outer shadow (weaker and cooler), the lit edge of glass (stays white, stronger). A yellow leans towards amber as it darkens, so gold stays gold.
  - **The switch is upside down on purpose.** The page has always run under the light colour scheme (`body` is `color-scheme: normal`), and the dark look had to stay exactly as it was. So the first colour of each pair, the one the light scheme shows, is the dark look's, and the light look is `color-scheme: dark` on `body`. Don't "tidy" this: turning it round means the dark look runs under the dark scheme, which changes every button and box that has no colour of its own.
  - **In the stylesheet** the colours are only rewritten while the light look is on (`skin` → `lightCss`, about 5ms, once): in the dark look the stylesheet is the text it always was. The light look's stylesheet is that text rewritten, then `LIGHT_EXTRA`.
  - **In inline styles and SVG colour attributes** they are rewritten always, in both looks, by the JSX factory `flowEl` (the three pragma lines at the very top of the file make every element go through it instead of React's own `jsx`). They have to be: a look changed in Settings can't wait for every part to be drawn again. In the dark look they come out as the same colours (read back from the page: the big figure `rgb(255, 93, 108)`, a day tile `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, a card `rgba(27, 30, 35, 0.58)`).
  - **Never name anything `flowEl`**, and don't go back to a short name: the factory was `h` for one build, and `CurRoll`'s own `const h = 17` was called in its place ("r is not a function", nothing drawn).
  - **What keeps the dark look** in the light one is pinned back to the light scheme (`LT_STAGE`): `.stage-bg`, `.acc`, `.open-panel`, `.launch-veil`, `.shot-view` (a trade's picture looked at), `.av` (a profile picture), and anything with `data-dk` (the quick log over the panels, the switch itself). **A new drawing whose colours are its own (a picture, an illustration) gets `data-dk`** or it is recoloured.
  - **By hand** (`LIGHT_EXTRA`, `ld(dark, light)`): the glass. A dark pane is a see-through fill lighter than the page, and so is a light one, which the rewriting can't know (it makes a white veil a faint dark one, right for a button, a well, a line). So `.glass`, `.glass-soft`, `.flow-card`, `.hero`, `.cal-wrap`, `.snav`, `.wr-tile` and the tiles' sheen are written there as white glass, inside `@scope (html) to (the stage)` so nothing before an app is touched and no rule gets stronger. The day tiles' and month boxes' fills and edges are pairs in `TILE`, the pop-ups' dim in `S.backdrop`.
  - **What the rewriting can't see**: a colour with `calc()` inside it (`rgba(0,0,0,calc(…))`: the tiles' sheen, done by hand), a colour a script works out and writes straight to the page or a canvas (only on the stage today), anything inside `url(…)`, and what stands between `LT-KEEP` and `LT-END` in the stylesheet (left alone on purpose: the switch).
  - The backdrop is `PAGE_BG_LT`: the same picture as `PAGE_BG` (`pageBgOf`), the same glows in the same places as pastels (sky blue, mint, lilac) on a pale cool grey. Still one picture of a fixed height.
  - **The way in and out**: the stage's backdrop (`.stage-bg`) gets `inapp` as the app is entered and, in the light look, cross-fades to the light backdrop under the greeting and the big year (a layer of its own, opacity only), so by the time it fades away the page behind is the same picture. Home again it fades in over the light page (0.36s).
- **The switch** (`ThemeSwitch`, `.th-sw`, in `SettingsPop`, so on every side; the row is called Appearance). After his picture of a day and night switch ("the button should be sun (yellow) and moon (dark blue)", "make the moon a crescent moon", "button slide animation should also be used here"): a pill of sky with a knob that slides across in the sliding switches' time and curve (0.34s, `--ease-soft`). Day: a yellow sun at the left, a blue sky going to yellow, clouds rising in. Night: a crescent moon at the right in a dark blue sky with six stars. Rings of light round the knob as in his picture. Transitions on transform and opacity only, so a press half-way turns it round. It looks the same in both looks.
- **Every switch with choices slides** (same day: "all the buttons should slide animation if theres options"): the four that still swapped their grey box on the spot are `SlideSeg` now: Developer options (On / Off), the finance currency, the trading currency shown, and By item / Single purchases in the wrapped. **Not changed, he has not been asked**: the option rows that are drawn another way and carry a colour of their own (the PnL analysis time frames, a trade's Win / Breakeven / Loss, Long / Short and time frame, the calculators' Long / Short, the chart pop-up's two buttons).
- **Checked** (the hidden engine, test data, never his entries):
  - The `light` journey: into finance in the dark look, the switch pressed on and off in Settings. Dark, light, dark again: the scheme on `body` `normal`, `dark`, `normal`; the big figure `rgb(255, 93, 108)`, `rgb(220, 59, 80)` and back; the key `light`, then `dark`; a mark put on the window beforehand still there (never loaded again). With `--light`: eight stills of the way in (the dark stage, the greeting in ink on the light backdrop, the year landing, the yearly panel).
  - Stills of the light look: finance with `look --light` (20: year, month, calendar, a day, every card, chart, wrapped, category, quick log, sidebar, split bill, settings), crypto with `tlook --light --side=crypto` (18, the trading panel among them: dark, as meant). No console error in any run.
  - What `flowEl` costs, in Node against React's own `jsx` (2 million elements each): 40ns more for an element with a style from `S`, 26ns with none, about 750ns with a style written in place holding two colours. A month of 1,600 elements: about 0.4ms more, in both looks. The page's month opening is 58 to 77ms.
  - **Not checked**: the stocks and forex sides in the light look (the same `TradeTracker` as crypto), every pop-up one by one (the journal view, the score pop-up, the introduction's cards never show in an app), MYR, lag in the app itself (no traced journey was run: he was using the app), his 120Hz display, the way home in motion, Reduce motion.
- **The liquid glass slider's number** (`--lg-k`, 14 October 2026) is in the light look's panes too: every white of `.glass`, `.glass-soft`, the cards, the hero and the tray in `LIGHT_EXTRA`, and the tiles' light fills in `TILE`, as `rgba(…, calc(a * var(--lg-k)))`. The rewriting can't see a colour with `calc()` in it, so **a dark rule given the number needs its light twin written by hand**. The category menu (`.cat-pop.glass`) and Quick log (`.ql.glass`) carry theirs as a `light-dark(dark, light)` pair in the rule itself (23 October 2026: given the number, they would otherwise have stayed dark in the light look).
- The save point from before it: `savepoints/before_light_mode.jsx`.
- **The softer light look** (20 October 2026, his words: "the light mode looks like a flashbang, make the colours and all the UI better to look at, but not too very bright that it hurts to look at"). Nothing is pure white any more; every layer is a step down, by numbers worked out in OKLCH:
  - The backdrop `PAGE_BG_LT`: a cool mist grey `#dce0e6` → `#cdd1d7` (was `#f4f6fa` → `#dfe4ec`), its glows at a little over half their strength.
  - The panes in `LIGHT_EXTRA` (`.glass`, `.glass-soft`, cards, hero, calendar, wrapped tiles) and the tiles in `TILE` are a light tone, `rgb(239,242,245)`, with less of it: a card comes out about `#eaedf1` (lightness 0.945; it was `#fefeff`, 0.997), a pop-up about 0.91 over the dimmed page. Lit edges, sheens and `--lg-lens` at a little over half. The sidebar `#e3e6eb` → `#d9dde2` (was white). Default ink `#1e2227`, not black; boxes `#eaedf1`, buttons `#e0e3e7`. The pop-ups' dim 0.28 (was 0.24).
  - `toLight` follows: a dark fill (a well, a button, a chart's track) becomes 0.9 … 0.94 (`fillL`; was 0.955 … 0.995), a line 0.84 down (was 0.9), a lit white edge at most 0.62 (was 0.95), a dark tinted fill a pale tint at 0.915 (was 0.95). Coloured text is deepened until it reads at 4.5 to 1 against a card (`against`, was 4 to 1 against white), the greys a hair darker (`inkL`).
  - Checked in Node with the file's own `toLight`: greys 4.4 to 1 or more on the page, the cards and the pop-ups; colours 4.6 on a card, 4.2 in a pop-up, 3.8 on the bare page (large figures there). No fill or background in the file comes out above 0.95 except small pieces (switch knobs, the day / night switch's cloud) and the stage, which stays dark.
- **Sliding switches clip what stands out of them** (`.wr-seg.slide { overflow: clip; overflow-clip-margin: 6px }`, 20 October 2026, his pictures of the settings at IDR and at MYR: "this box extends when switching currencies, and its not smooth cause of the slider on the bottom"). `SlideSeg`'s box is as wide as the whole switch and is moved to the choice, so on a right-hand choice it stood out past the switch by that much; a pop-up that scrolls counted it as content, grew a sideways scroll bar (11px, the app's own) and got taller, a little more each frame of the slide. Found by a check pressing every switch on every side (finance: settings 557.8 → 568.8px on MYR and on Developer options Off; the spending rule pop-up opened 70px too wide; the split bill 664 → 675 on % / Rp). After the clip: no height change and no sideways scroll in any frame. The margin keeps a button's focus outline whole (2px at 2px).
- **The day / night switch cross-fades the page** (20 October 2026, the motion pass): the look changed in one frame. It goes through `glassFade` (the view transition of the glass slider, 0.45s) with `flushSync` inside, so React has put the new look in place before the engine takes its picture of the new page; without `flushSync` the fade would run to the old look and the new one would jump in after it. Reduce motion: at once. Checked with the `motionpass` journey.
