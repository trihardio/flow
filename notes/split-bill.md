# Split bill

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Split bill** (`BillSplit`, `splitBill`, `billApi`, `.sb-*`; finance side; 11 October 2026: "a calculator for calculating split bill with people", then "an option for my bill to be input for expenses (also add option for changing Rp and desc and category)"):
  - Opened by the people button on a day's Expenses row (beside the receipt) or by "Split bill" on top of the finance sidebar, under Quick add. It is the settings' pop-up, wider.
  - Boxes: the bill, Service % and Tax % (both may stay empty), the number of people (− / +, 2 to 20), and a line per person: a name (the first is "Me") and what that person had on their own.
  - The sum (`splitBill`): what somebody had on their own stays with them, the rest of the bill is shared evenly, then service and tax go on everybody's part alike, service on the bill and tax on the bill with service. Whole rupiah. Own items that come to more than the bill are refused in one red line.
  - **What a person had, item by item** (same day: "if i buy 2 food at diff price and my other friend does too make it so i can calculate them clerly"): a receipt button on every person's line unfolds a list of their own things, the day panel's own (`PartLines`, reporting to the pop-up through `linesApi`). With items listed, the person's amount box is what they come to (dashed, "total"); the button shuts and opens the list and keeps what is in it.
  - **With no bill typed, the bill is what everybody's own things come to**: each then pays for exactly what they had, plus service and tax (the Bill label says so). A bill typed on top of that shares the rest evenly. The Pays figure's tooltip says what it is made of.
  - The pop-up no longer eases its height (`useEaseHeight`): the lists in it fold by themselves, and the two fought (the bottom was cut off for a moment).
  - It shows what each person pays, the total, and "Each pays" (or "My share" when the parts differ).
  - **My share as an expense**: an amount box that holds his share until he types his own figure (which stands until the share itself changes), a description, the five categories in one quiet field (the one read from the description is lit faintly until he picks), and "Add to today" or "Add to 10 Oct": the day whose panel is open, else today. The same thing can't be added twice in a row. With no description it is filed as "Split bill".
  - **"I paid"** (`owers`, `lend`, `lentSig`, `billApi.owed`, `.sb-owe`; 13 October 2026, from the critique's list): a quiet button under "Add to today" puts what each of the others pays into Debts as owed to him, one debt per person, named after them and the description ("Adi · dinner"). The same split is not put there twice in a row. Checked with the `safety` journey: 100.000 between two gave one debt of 50.000, "Person 2 · dinner", owed. Not built: the other way round (somebody else paid, he owes his share).
  - Not built, not asked for: keeping the boxes after it is shut.
  - **13 October 2026, "make the split bill more advanced"** (`BillRate`, `SB_HOW`, `shared`, `.sb-rate`, `.sb-unit`, `.sb-item`, `.sb-chip`, `.sb-hand`, `.sb-inote`):
    - **Service and tax are each a % or an amount** ("option to change from % to a fixed Rp rate (currency change dont forget)"): a small sliding switch beside each name, % or the currency shown (Rp, or RM with its sen). An amount is put on everybody's part by its size, exactly as a % is (20.000 on a bill of 200.000 is 10%); tax in % is still on the bill with service. Each kind keeps its own figure, so switching back brings it back.
    - **A thing shared by some** ("i buy 400rb pizza (one box) … calculate by how many people ate that pizza and either split evenly or manually"): "+ Shared item" under the people adds a line: its price, what it is, **Evenly / By hand**, and a chip per person to tick who had it (everybody to begin with; somebody added later has it too). Evenly, whoever had it pays the same. **By hand, a box per person who had it: what is typed is what that one pays, and whoever is left empty shares what remains evenly** (the grey figure in the box, "Rp 100.000 · auto"). So nothing has to add up by his own sum; only more than the thing costs, or all typed and less than it costs, is refused, in a red line under that item. A line under the item says what it comes to ("3 people · Rp 133.333 each").
    - With no bill typed the bill is still what everybody had, added up, the shared things included; a bill typed on top shares the rest evenly among all, as before.
    - **What people pay now adds up to the total, to the last rupiah** (in ringgit to the sen): every part is rounded down and the rupiah left over go, one each, to the parts that lost most (the first person on a tie). Rounded one by one, as before, 54.862,5 and 60.637,5 came to a rupiah more than the 115.500 they share. So 100.000 between three is 33.334, 33.333, 33.333 and the tile says "My share", not "Each pays".
