# The Weekly expenses card: planned expenses, other weeks, a day's note

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Planned expenses, in the Weekly expenses card** (`WeekPlans`, `cleanPlans`, `planDays`, `planApi`, `plans`, `.wp-*`; 11 October 2026: "project expenses on this week … i want to get haircut this week and i wanna eat at a restaurant", "it will also be calculated on the weekly expense chart, make sure its the future and not the past, can be the present. have options for choosing dates. if its end of week then have it be input next week i guess").
  - Under the chart: an amount, what for, a + button, and the day as one quiet field of choices: Today, then each day to the end of this week (Monday first). **On the week's last day the choices are Today and all seven days of next week.** The first day after today is chosen to begin with. No day in the past can be chosen, and none is offered.
  - The plans to come are listed soonest first, **from the week on the chart on** (14 October 2026: with a week ahead on the chart, the plans of the weeks before it were listed under it with nothing to say so, while only those after it said "· later"; he sent a picture of October's plans under the week of 30 Nov – 6 Dec as a bug). On this week, and on a week behind, that is from today on, as before. Each with a tick (**it happened: it becomes an expense of today**, filed as the quick log files one, and leaves the plan) and a bin.
  - **A plan is not spending**: `{ id, amount, desc, date }` under `plans` in `pnl-calendar-idr`, in no total, category or score. It shows on the weekly chart, and since 12 October 2026 the month's projection counts it (see Projection), as a long-dashed line (its look: see the rework below) that carries on from the last point drawn, each day at what it holds plus what is planned for it, a hollow dot on each planned day (today's is an upright on top of today's point); the scale grows to hold it, the note under the pointer says "+80Rb planned", and the card's line says "· +80Rb planned" for the week on the chart. The chart shows the week of today, or of the day whose panel is open: a plan for next week shows there once a day of that week is opened, or from Monday on.
  - A plan whose day has passed is dropped when the entries are read (`cleanPlans`) and counts nowhere from midnight on. Plans go into the backup and come back on import (an import replaces them, like debts).
  - A module-level `memo` component with its own state, fed through a ref, like Debts.
  - **Its look, reworked 12 October 2026** (his words: "the input box should be red, the selected date should have a dotted red outline on the dates boxes, treat it as a real expense input where i can go to the next week on expenses chart and there will be a line for the expense. the lines for planned expenses are long dotted", and "dotted lines are dashed lines", with a picture of long dashes). **A plan looks like an expense, and dashed means planned**:
    - The amount box is the expenses' red one (`accent="red"`, and a faint red border at rest so it reads as red before it is typed in); the amounts in the list are red; the card's "+150Rb planned" is red. The first version was blue-grey throughout: he did not want that.
    - **The day chosen stands in a dashed red outline that slides to the day pressed** (`.wp-days`, `.wp-on`, `--at`; "make the animation slide when choosing another date"). The days are boxes of one width (72px, 2px apart), so the outline is one piece of that width moved by transform and never resized. It was `SlideSeg` for an hour, with a solid outline: its two clipped pieces can't carry a dashed border (the dashes of the two would lie on each other out of step). The days keep their numbers ("Tue 13"): he asked for them off and took that back in the next message.
    - **The planned line is the expenses' own line in long dashes**: the same colour by height (`url(#weekLine)`: green, yellow from 75% of the limit, red over it), 13 on and 7 off, square ends; the dot on a planned day has a dashed ring in that colour. Don't make it dotted or another colour again.
  - **The weekly chart can be stepped to other weeks** (`weekAt`, `weekStep`, `weekSpot`, `.wk-step`; same message): two arrows beside the compare symbol at its top right. It goes back to the week it would show whenever the day or the month looked at changes. A week with no day in a year the app has is not offered. The card is only put there afresh when that home week changes (`runKey={weekData.home}`), not on a step: the week's own marks are keyed by the week instead and draw in again while the axes stand, and what is typed in the planned box stays.
    - **With a week ahead on the chart, the days offered are that week's seven** (`planDays(now, week)`), so a plan can be put on any day of a week he has stepped to and its line shows there at once.
  - Checked with the `pick` journey on Monday 12 October 2026 (a weekday: Today to Sun 18, Tue chosen to begin with): the outline's place read every frame (it slid from Tue to Fri in about 0.35s and ended on the day to the tenth of a pixel); a plan two days on (the dashed path with its gradient, one dashed dot); a step on a week with 70.000 typed (still in the box, the days Mon 19 to Sun 25), a plan there and its line; a step back to last week and its real line; this week again.
- **A day's note on the weekly chart opens when pressed** (`WeekPin`, `weekPin`, `shutWeekPin`, `dayNote`, `.glass-tip.pin`, `.wkp-*`; 12 October 2026: "when hovering a date on weekly expense and then clicking the hovered ui, the ui expands and below the …Rb show a list of expenses for that day and when clicking outside the box the ui disappears and becomes a normal hover over ui").
  - It is the note itself, the same glass, wider, with the date in full, the figure, and under it that day's expenses (the category's symbol, the name, the amount) and what is planned for it (a clock, in pale red); "Nothing spent that day" when there is neither. Only a day of more than six lines scrolls (`.wkp-list.many`: with the rule on every list a scroll bar showed beside three rows).
  - While it is open the notes do not follow the pointer. A press anywhere outside it, Esc (which goes no further) or the window resized shuts it on a short fade, and the notes follow the pointer again (the day under the pointer shows at the next movement: `onMouseMove`, not only on entering a day).
  - It comes in with the note's own pop, like every note. With no room above its point it stands under it, and it never reaches past the chart's sides (both read once from the layout before it is first drawn). Its entrance holds only its first frame (`animation-fill-mode: backwards`): held at its last, as the plain note's is, the way out could not be seen.
  - Its state is a hover hub of its own, so opening it redraws only the note, not the panel.
  - Checked with the `pick` journey: pressed open on a day with three expenses (all three listed with their symbols), the pointer taken to another day meanwhile (nothing changed), a press outside (shut 0.12s on, gone 0.6s on), the note of a planned day by pointer, that one pressed (under its point, the plan listed), Esc (shut, the month still open). Not checked: a day of more than six lines, MYR.
  - Checked with the `plans` journey on Sunday 11 October 2026 (so the next-week case): the eight choices, a plan for Monday (listed, not on this week's chart), one for today (the upright and its dot, "+80Rb planned"), the tick (an 80.000 expense on today, the plan gone), the bin, and a past plan put into the saved data by hand gone after a reload. Not checked: a weekday (the choices then end on Sunday), MYR, the dashed line running over several days ahead (drawn by the same code as the upright; seen for one day only).
