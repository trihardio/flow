# Logging on the finance side

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Quick log** (`QuickLog`, `parseQuick`, `quickNumber`, `.ql-*`; finance side; 7 October 2026, the review's second critical finding): ⌘N anywhere on the finance side, or the sidebar's top button. One line of text read as it is typed, with what it will add written under it; Enter adds it to today and the box stays open for the next one; Enter on the empty line or Esc shuts it.
  - A line may hold, in any order: an amount, what it was for, a `+` for income (Tab switches too), "yesterday" / "kemarin". `kopi 25` · `25rb kopi` · `1,5jt sewa` · `+5jt gaji` · `kemarin bensin 50`. In rupiah a bare number under 1000 is thousands, as everywhere in the app; rb / k and jt / juta say so outright. The first number in the line is the amount.
  - **The hint under the line names the other kind** (14 October 2026, his words: "if income is selected, change to Tab outcome, same thing goes the other way around"): "Tab income · Esc close" on an expense, "Tab outcome · Esc close" on an income. "Outcome" is his word; the switch beside it still says "Expense".
  - **Often**: the names entered at least twice in the last 90 days, most frequent first, each with the amount it most often had; one press (or ⌘1–6) adds it to today. **A plural s does not make another name** (11 October 2026: "snacks and snack should just be 1"): the two are counted together and the chip carries the spelling used most. Nothing else is merged there (the breakdown lists' `CANON` is not used: it would make one chip of gocar and goride).
  - The foot says today's spending against the daily limit and what is left of it.
  - It files through `quickApi.current.put(kind, rupiah, desc, days back)`, which the yearly panel's quick-add row uses as well (`add`). While it is open no key and no wheel reaches the panels behind, like the settings pop-up. A module-level `memo` component with its own state.
  - **A capsule "Log ⌘N" on the finance month** (`.log-pill`, `.log-side`; 12 October 2026: "logging an expense, the thing done many times a day, has no visible way in on this screen"): in the strip under the title, in what was empty room just left of the daily limit. - **A logged row is moved to another day by dragging its bar** (`rowDrag`, `moveApi`, `calGrid`, `.row-grip`, `.dnd-chip`, `.cal-ring`, `DND_*`; finance day panel, expenses and incomes; 11 October 2026: "drag and drop (mac style)" by "the gray bar inbetween expense rp text and description", "all the transitions … very smooth and seamless. no lag", "make no mistakes"). For entries put on the wrong day: no retyping.
  - The grey bar between the amount and the name is the handle (as it was at rest; under the pointer a lighter, thicker bar and the open hand). Held and moved 4px, the row dims where it is and a small copy (amount, bar, name) grows out of the bar and stays under the pointer, its own bar on the spot taken hold of. A white ring glides to the day it is over (within half a gap of a tile; never the row's own day). Let go there: the copy flies into that day, the row folds away, and the entry is on the other day. Let go anywhere else, on its own day, or Esc (which goes no further): it glides back.
  - **The entry goes over as it is**: same id, amount, name, category, symbol and items; an expense is appended to the other day's list, an income only has its `day` changed. A no-spend mark on the day it lands on goes, as when an expense is added; a day left with nothing loses its key. The panel stays on the day it was on, so several can be moved one after the other. Only within the month on screen.
  - **None of the drag is React state** (`rowDrag` is plain script at module level): the copy and the ring move by transform, written once a frame at most; the dimmed row and the held bar are classes put on by hand; the days' places are read once as the drag begins and kept as places on the page, so scrolling reads nothing. Near the top or bottom edge of the window the page scrolls by itself (`DND_EDGE`, `DND_SCROLL`). The page hears of it only at the drop, two frames on, so its own work can't hold up the copy's take-off.
  - A moved row fades from its dimmed state (`removing[id] === "move"`, `.row-wrap.gone`); a deleted one still pops (`=== true`). The move is done by `moveApi.current.land` 270ms later, always the one of the latest drawing, so it reads what is saved at that moment. **The two deletes read `latestData.current` for the same reason**: their timers used what was on screen when the bin was pressed, and a row moved or removed in that quarter second came back.
  - While a row is held the day tiles take no pointer (`.cal-dnd .cell`), so no tile lifts and no tile's note comes up under the copy. Don't put a rule with ` *` under a class that goes on at the start of a drag (a cursor for the whole page, say): it restyles the page at the very moment of the lift. The cursor comes from the bar, which holds the pointer.
  - Checked with `tools/perf` journey `dragmove` (`--fresh`; `--shots` for stills): an expense onto a day with a no-spend mark, one with items, an income, the last one out of a day, a second move from the day it went to, and one with the calendar out of sight (held at the top edge, the page scrolled 775px to it): each arrived unchanged to the character.
