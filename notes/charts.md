# Charts

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Chart pop-up browsing:** the chart has its own period (`chartAt`, read through `cY` / `cM`), so stepping through months or years in it never moves the panel behind; closing it leaves the panel where it was.
  - **Compare** (`CmpButton`, `GhostLine`, `chartCmp`, `chartCmpData`; both sides, 4 October 2026): a borderless button at the bottom right of the pop-up puts the period before or after the chart's behind it as a ghost line: the year before / after on the yearly chart, the month before / after on the monthly one. It goes off → the one before → the one after → off ("Compare", "vs 2025", "vs Nov 2026"); a period with nothing in it isn't offered, and with neither the button is dimmed.
    - The ghost is a dark slate blue (`CMP_INK`, #7d93a8) at 55% opacity, drawn before the chart's own marks so it sits behind them: he asked for a colour that feels like grey without being grey, "like a ghost behind the main chart lines". The first one was a soft violet, which he "really" didn't like: keep it discreet and dark.
    - How it moves (his words: nothing restarts, "everything is smooth and seamless"): pressing Compare never replays the chart's own line. The ghost draws in from the left like any chart line (`.cmp-line.line-sweep`), while the chart's own marks ease to their place on the new scale (`.ch-morph`: a CSS transition on the path's `d`, on `y`, `height`, `cy`; `.ch-slide` for the zero line and the limit marker) and the scale's numbers count to their new values (`CountNum … still`, `CMP_MS`). The marks are keyed by the period only (`pk`), never by the comparison, or they would be put there afresh and draw in again.
    - Turning it off: the scale goes back, and the ghost stays mounted for `CMP_MS` (`useGone`), fading as the chart's own line settles over it, then is removed. The button's words roll letter by letter (`RollWord`).
    - **Weekly expenses** has its own (`weekCmp`, `weekCmpData`, `.wk-cmp`): a symbol at the top right of the chart, in line with the ends of the two axes, that goes off → the week before → the week after → off. Both compares start off.
    - It is on the chart's own scale (the scale grows to hold it) and in the chart's mode: a running total on the cumulative chart, each day's or month's own figure on the others. It follows along when the chart is stepped.
  - Step with the ‹ › arrows right after the title (invisible until the pointer is on the title, like `.month-nav`), the ← → keys, or the scroll wheel.
- **Scroll wheel steps** (the year stacks and the chart pop-ups, on both sides): one step per swipe (`wheelSwipe`). A trackpad keeps sending wheel events after the fingers leave it, longer with a third finger resting on it; the old half-second lock ran out during that glide and stepped twice (2026 → 2028, which he reported). Don't go back to a timed lock.
  - The first fix waited for the wheel to fall quiet, which left a second swipe dead while the first one's glide was still running: he had to move the pointer (that ends a glide) to scroll again, and it bothered him a lot.
  - Now: a glide only ever dies away, so a new swipe begins when the wheel has been quiet for `WHEEL_QUIET_MS` (0.6s) or when the events grow again (`WHEEL_SURGE` times what the stream had died down to, and `WHEEL_SURGE_PX` more), though not within `WHEEL_AGAIN_MS` of the step.
  - Tests: `node tools/wheel-test.mjs` feeds the rule trackpad-like streams (one swipe with long, uneven or broken glides must be one step; a second swipe 0.4s to 3s later must count). The streams are shaped like a trackpad's, not recorded from his.
  - Years and months both stop at the ends of `YEARS` (`monthAfter`). Clicking a data point still closes the chart and opens that day or month.
  - Only the data marks are keyed by period (`pk`), so a step redraws the line or bars and leaves the axes still. `.ch-moved` makes that redraw quicker than the first draw.
- Charts:
  - Grey for no-data spans, with a hard colour switch at each data point.
  - Lines start at the y-axis, at 90% of their first point's value (`CHART_LEAD`), so they come in with a slight rise. From zero it was a wall from the corner up to January, which he found weird.
  - The x-axis stays one unbroken white line: a level that falls on it draws no grid line, and the dashed zero line is drawn only when zero is above the axis. Both used to be drawn over it (he noticed the axis was "not fully white").
  - Weekly line: green below 75% of the daily limit, yellow up to the limit, red above it.
  - **The weekly chart's axis** (`WeekBox`, `--wk`, `.wk-ax`, `niceTicks`; 12 October 2026: in an empty week "the axis figures are the biggest text in the card", "use round steps and keep the limit's own dashed line"):
    - The chart is one drawing 720 units wide stretched to the card's width, so text written at 11 came out at about 18px in his window. `WeekBox` reads the box's width (and again when it changes) and writes `--wk`, what a pixel is in the drawing's units, straight to the element (no state). The axis text is `calc(11px * var(--wk))`: 11px on screen at any width, the projection chart's size. The day names came down with the figures.
    - How far the text stands from the axes is still in the drawing's units (12 left of the y-axis, 11 under the x-axis), because today's ring is: on a Monday it sits on the corner of the two axes. Set in pixels (9 and 15), the ring lay over the "0" and over "Mon".
    - The steps were a third, two thirds and the whole of the scale's top, and the top is 115% of the biggest thing on the chart: in an empty week the daily limit, so 56, 113 and 172Rb for a limit of 150. They are round now (`niceTicks`, about four, round in the currency shown: a round rupiah step is no round ringgit one) and the scale ends on the last of them: 0, 50, 100 and 200Rb, with the limit's dashed line and its own figure at 150 (a step within 13 units of the limit's line gives way to it, as before). The figures are written in full (`fmt`: "1,5jt"); the coarse form they had (`fmtCoarse`: "1jt" for 1,5jt) is gone, nothing else used it.
    - The card's line says "Nothing spent yet · 12 Oct – 18 Oct" where it said "0 · …"; for a week that lies wholly before today "Nothing spent" (`weekData.over`): the "yet" would be wrong there. He asked for the first only.
    - Checked with the `week` journey (new; `--shots`) on a copy of his entries, an empty week: every axis text 11.0px on screen (the card 1202px wide), the steps 0, 50Rb, 100Rb, 200Rb and 150Rb on the limit's line, the header's words, and a still. Not seen: a week with spending in it, Compare (the scale's figures counting), MYR, a narrower window, a past empty week.
  - Income bars (chart pop-up): each bar has a soft top-to-bottom gradient, light green to a slightly deeper green (`incBarG`), never dark. The biggest income of the period uses the lighter `incBarTopG`.
- **The chart pop-up's modes have words** (22 October 2026, the options pass): finance's buttons read "+ Income", "− Spending" and "+/− PnL", the trading charts' "+/− Running total" and the bars "Each month" / "Each day" (`.mode-w`); the symbols kept their colours. Alone, "+", "−" and "+/−" meant nothing to somebody new.
