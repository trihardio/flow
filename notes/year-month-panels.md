# The yearly and monthly panels

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Yearly panel layout:** the year stack fills the window from the header down to the footer (`.year-viewport`, `--yv`), so the footer sits at the bottom and the month boxes grow with the window (three equal rows in `--yb`). `YEAR_PEEK` px of the neighbouring years stay visible, dimmed: the first year sits at the top with the next one showing below, the last year at the bottom with the previous one above, and a year in between is centred with half of the peek on each side. Rassam found the old fixed-size stack left the bottom of the window empty.
- **The yearly panel's month boxes say what they hold** (13 October 2026, from a critique of the panel he asked for; he chose "all five", and for the quick-add row "keep it, make it readable"; `yc-w`, `yc-lim`, `.ycell.later`, `yearSparks`, `tools/perf` journey `year`):
  - **Each figure is named** (finance): "1,72jt left" or "122,8Rb short" (income minus spending; the word follows the month's own figure, not the count on its way), then "270,7Rb spent" with no minus. They were two bare numbers. The trading boxes already said theirs ("6 trades · 17% win") and are as they were.
  - **A note under the pointer** (finance, the day tile's own `.cell-tip`): the month, what came in and went out, and "Against the daily limit +179,3Rb", the figure the month's own page leads with. **The box is coloured by income against spending, the month's page is scored against the daily limit**: the note is where the two meet. Only on the year in view; under the box in the first row, over it in the others, so it stays inside the stack. **Solid, no blur**: it is the first screen, and a blur would be built on its first showing. `.ycell` no longer cuts off what reaches past it (`overflow: visible`, as the day tiles).
  - **Every month of a year is drawn on one scale** (finance): each line was stretched to its own highest day, so October with 270Rb stood as tall as August with 4,42jt. The scale is the year's highest day **by its square root**: on a straight scale one very big day presses every other month flat on the floor. The lines themselves are as he asked for them. The trading side's lines (each month's running result on its own range) were not looked at and are as they were.
  - **An empty month is its name alone** (all four sides): the centred "·" is gone, as on the day tiles the day before. **A month still to come with nothing on it is a step fainter** (`.later`: its fill, its edge, its light and its name), so the months not yet lived no longer look like the ones left empty. Never by the box's own opacity: its entrance (`tileIn`) ends on 1 and would jump back.
  - **The quick-add row's hint is readable** (`.qadd-ph`, all four sides): #b0b8c0, which through the row's 70% is about 4.7 to 1; it was a placeholder's grey and came to 2.4 to 1, the faintest text on the panel. The row itself, its 70% and its button are as they were (the button already says what it does under the pointer).
  - **The year in the header is a step smaller, with a sparkle beside it** (all four sides; asked again and he chose both: "2026" and the figure stood at one height on one line and read as one string, and a press on "2026" opens the wrapped with no sign that it does). The pixel year is 40px (`S.yearBig`; it was 48: a font pixel is 5px now), its foot on the line it stood on, so the figure leads. The wrapped's own sparkle (`.yb-wr`, the sidebar's symbol) stands at its top right, grey at rest and the wrapped's gold under the pointer; it is part of the same thing that is pressed.
    - The normal-font twin the intro's big year lands on (`.year-big .yb-n`) is written in the year's own size now (em: 58px, -8.5px and 42px were written for a 48px year), and the last digit's roll is 40 high. - **`YEARS`** is dynamic: the current year + 2, plus years with data, plus the viewed year.
  - Month stepping never leaves it (`monthAfter`, used by the monthly panel's arrows and keys and by the chart pop-up): past December it goes to the next year in the list, and at the first January or the last December it stops, with that arrow greyed out. Rassam does not want to reach years that don't exist.
- **Month title flicker** (`monthFlick`, `.month-big`): the month name does the header year's normal font → pixel font flicker, letter by letter, with the same cells (`.yb-cell` / `.yb-px` / `.yb-n`) and keyframes.
  - Opening the monthly panel: it holds the normal font for `YEAR_FLICK_DELAY_MS` first, then flickers into the pixel font.
  - Switching months is pixel font to pixel font, no normal font in between: each cell flickers several times between the old month's letter (`.yb-old`) and the new one before settling, in the header year's own rhythms (`ybN` for the one that goes, `ybP` for the one that stays, 1.25–1.65s per letter), starting a little later for each letter to the right. About 2s in all. Shorter versions (0.6s, then 1.1s with a single soft flicker) were "just flashing" to him. A longer old name fades out past the end of the new one. Rassam found the earlier version (old pixel → new normal → new pixel, random timing) "way too janky"; keep it orderly and unhurried.
  - In the pixel font "t" sits one font pixel (3px) to the right in its box, so each "t" and what follows is pulled back by 3px (`.tt`, `monthTightShift`). He noticed the gap in August, September and October.
  - It is rolled in a layout effect, so the new title never shows in the pixel font for a frame first. `id` is part of the cell keys, which restarts the animation on every switch.
  - The normal-font letters are spread evenly over the width of the pixel word (`monthTwinShift`, measured once per month name, handed over as `--nx`); left in their 24px cells they looked unevenly spaced.

- Yearly panel header: after the wallet → year intro, the year digits flicker from the normal font into the pixel font. They wait `YEAR_FLICK_DELAY_MS` (0.4s) after the panel appears; starting at once felt too fast.
- Year arrows (`.ynav-btn`): at the first or last year the arrow fades to its greyed-out state over 0.32s, and back. Rassam noticed it cutting straight to grey.
- Every ✕ (`.x-btn`), the calculators' gear and the × on an income chip (`.chip-x`) light up under the pointer.
- Month panel header: the title is the name of the month being viewed ("October"), in the pixel font (Press Start 2P, 24px); clicking it opens the month's wrapped. The ‹ › month arrows on the right are always on show since 7 October 2026 (`.month-nav`); they were invisible until the pointer was on the month switcher.
- **Card order on the finance month panel:** Weekly expenses, Money flow, Goals (he asked for Goals under Money flow).
- **Cards open when they are scrolled to** (he asked for "all ui" to animate on scrolling down): a card pops in when 18% of it shows (`FoldCard`), and its content is put there afresh at that moment, which starts its animations and count-ups. Money flow is tall, so its four parts (the flow chart, the score, the projection, the insights) each wait for their own turn (`Reveal`: hidden but holding their room until their top is 12% of the window up from the bottom edge, then mounted afresh with a short fade). Folding and opening a card with its arrow starts the parts on screen again; the ones below wait.
