# Home: the trading dashboard

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`. The trading tracker it sits in is `notes/trading.md`.

- **What he asked** (20 October 2026, with eight reference pictures: Stakent, qclay, dstudio, bato.dev, Fundex and three wallet / portfolio apps): "a home dashboard type of thing where there are multiple UI boxes for different information", "just like apple widgets where i can resize and move around", "a on off snapping button", "each boxes can be added / removed just like Apple iOS 26 widgets", "colorful though still keep the original grey liquid glass theme, use friendly and soft colours". Asked before it was built, he chose: **the first screen** of a trading side (the yearly panel one press away), **all three sides** (crypto, IDX, forex, each with its own layout), and **live prices once a minute**.
- **Where it is** (`src/App.jsx`, the block "Home: the trading dashboard" just above `TradeTracker`): `TradeDash` (the board), `DashWidget` and one component per widget (`DgBal` … `DgActs`, listed in `DG_BODY`), `DgGallery` (Add a widget), the board's arithmetic (`dgSettle`, `dgArrange`, `dgFit`, `dgNear`, `dgCls`, `dgClean`), the figures (`dgTally`, `dgSeries`, `dgNames`, `dgRecent`, `dgStreak`, `dgWatch`), the styles (the block `HOME: the trading dashboard` in the stylesheet, its light pane in `LIGHT_EXTRA`, calm keyframes under Reduce motion).
- **In the tracker**: `view` starts on `"home"` (it was `"year"`). Home → yearly panel: the Calendar button, the Months widget, the sidebar's Year. Back: the **Home** capsule beside Log on the yearly header, or the sidebar's Home row (`id: "dash"`; `"home"` was already the crocodile's way to the opening screen). The widgets reach the tracker through one ref (`dashApi`: `save`, `opt`, `go`, `setBalance`), so the board is not drawn again with every render of the panel. On Home a digit typed opens the quick log as on the yearly panel, and the footer's test data fills the year.
- **The board**: 12 columns of square cells; a cell is a twelfth of the board's width, its 16px gap included (`pitch`, 102.7px at a 1440px window). Sizes are iOS's: small 2×2, medium 4×2, large 4×4, extra large 8×4; each widget lists the ones it comes in (`DG_W[id].sizes`).
  - **Snap on** (the default): places and sizes lock to the cells and to the widget's own sizes, and every widget rises into any room above it. **Snap off**: a widget stays exactly where it is put (to a hundredth of a cell) and takes any size from its smallest up to 12×8; nothing rises. Turning snap on again rounds every widget to a cell and its nearest size. Either way nothing overlaps: what is in the way moves down, and when a single widget is in the way of a move it takes the moved one's old place, if it fits there (a swap).
  - A widget with snap off can be any size: it is laid out as the biggest of its sizes that fits in it (`dgCls`).
  - With snap on, a gap can stay under a short widget when the one below is too wide to rise into it (the PnL widget made large leaves one under Balance, because the Equity curve is 8 wide). That is the rule, not a fault; he moves something into it.
- **Editing**: the Edit widgets button, or a press held still on a widget for 0.48s (`DG_HOLD_MS`; it shrinks a little while held), and the same press goes on as a drag, as on iOS. In edit mode: drag to move, the arc at the bottom right to resize (an outline shows the new size until the release), the round − at the top left to remove (with Undo for 6s; Reset puts every widget back as it came, also with Undo), Add widget, the Snap to grid switch, Done. Esc or a press on the board's empty space leaves it; Esc during a drag puts the widget back. With the keyboard: Tab to a widget, the arrows move it a cell, + and − change its size, Delete removes it.
  - **While the pointer moves nothing goes through React**: the dragged widget, the others making room (they ease in 0.38s) and the outline of where it will land are written to the page directly (`step`, `place`); the layout is saved once, on release. The window's pointer listeners are set once and read the latest of everything from a ref (`live`). After a release every widget is written to where it ends, and React then writes the same values.
  - Near the window's top or bottom edge the page scrolls by itself while dragging (`behavior: "instant"`: the page scrolls smoothly otherwise).
- **Saved** in the side's prefs (`pnl-crypto-prefs`, `pnl-idx-prefs`, `pnl-fx-prefs`) as `dash: { snap, items: [{ k, id, x, y, w, h, o }] }` (`o`: the widget's period or time frame), so each account and each side has its own, and it is not in a backup (a preference, like the currency). `null`: the dashboard as it comes (`DG_START`, placed in reading order). What is read back goes through `dgClean`: widgets this side doesn't have are dropped (forex has no Live prices, stocks no Long vs Short), sizes and places are kept inside the board, keys made unique, overlaps resolved. Nothing is saved before the prefs are read (the board only shows once they are).
- **The widgets** (each with a soft colour of its own: `DG_W[id].hue`): Balance (the balance and 30 days of it; set here when there is none, typed in the side's own unit, as in PnL analysis), PnL (Today / Week / Month / Year / All time, against the period before, made vs lost; large: a bar a day or a month), Win rate (a ring of wins, losses and break-evens, the figure in the pixel type; profit factor, average win and loss, per trade), Equity curve (7 / 30 / 90 days or the year so far; point at it to read a day), Calendar (this month as dots; a day pressed opens it), Months (this year's months as bars; one pressed opens it), Coins / Stocks / Pairs (best names by net PnL), Recent trades (extra large: a table), Live prices, Streak (green trading days in a row, as the wrapped counts them), Today (with Log trade), Long vs Short / Buy vs Sell, Shortcuts (Log trade, Calculator, Calendar, this month's Wrapped, and since 20 October 2026 Exchange: the money exchanger, see `notes/exchanger.md`). Exchange (the money exchanger's rows to type in, at live rates; see `notes/exchanger.md`). A chip with ⇕ on a widget is its period: a press moves it on, and it is kept with that widget.
  - Every figure is from his trades, deposits and balance with the tracker's own sums (`tally`, `cryptoMonth`, `cryptoYear`); nothing is made up. A result is green when it gained and red when it lost; the widget's own colour is only its glow, icon, line and the avatars.
  - The names are coloured discs with their letters (`DgAv`): no logos, nothing is fetched to draw one.
- **Live prices**: the names traded most over the last 90 days, then the market's best known ones, six at most (`dgWatch`). Asked one after another through `window.flowFX.quote` (the calculator's own source, `QUOTE_SOURCES`) once a minute (`DG_QUOTE_MS`), only while the widget is on screen and the window is showing, never in the gallery's preview. Answers are remembered in the prefs as the calculator's are; offline the last ones show with a grey dot. Forex has no price source, so no such widget there. Stocks: about 10 minutes behind the exchange, as the widget says.
- **Look**: grey glass panes as the cards, without a backdrop blur (widgets move, and a blur under something that moves is drawn again every frame; over the page's soft backdrop a blur shows next to nothing). A glow of the widget's colour from its top-left corner (22% in the dark look, 20% in the light one). Rounded corners (26px) on purpose: he asked for iOS widgets, while the rest of the trading side keeps its square terminal corners. Only transform and opacity are animated; the lifted widget's deeper shadow is a layer that fades in. The light look's pane is written by hand in `LIGHT_EXTRA`; the avatars and the shortcut tiles carry `data-dk` (their colours are their own).
- **Checked** (20 October 2026, the `dash` journey in `tools/perf`, a fresh test profile, never his entries; the hidden engine, not his 120Hz display): on crypto a held drag onto the neighbour (a swap), a resize from medium to large by the corner, snap off with a free drag and a free resize (2.52 cells wide), snap on again, a removal and its Undo, a widget added from the gallery, a period chip, a calendar day opening its month, the way back from the yearly panel; after each the saved layout and every widget standing where it says (all did); then IDX and forex with test data, and forex in the light look.   - **Not checked**: lag on his Mac (no traced run), live prices answering (the hidden run has no internet), MYR / IDR shown on crypto, a narrow window (cells shrink with it; the big figures scale with `--u`).
  - **Not done**: `public/warm-seed.json` has no Home screens yet, so the very first opening of Home on a fresh install builds its graphics programs then; after that the warm-up files and warms it like any screen.
