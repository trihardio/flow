## Timetable from a picture, second round (23 October 2026)
His words: "the picture ai is not very perfect … ive tested it with a couple example screenshots and it did not detect classes, meanwhile those are real timetables from the internet". The two (his Desktop, 18.40 and 18.44): a phone's photo of a university portal's week (myUNSW) taken at a slant, and a sheet of hour rows ("8-9" … "10-11") with merged cells, no times inside. Vision read both well; the reader found nothing in either. Now 64 of 64 (`tools/ocr/test.mjs` on copies of them; their expectations are in the test, the pictures are not in the project).
- **Hour rows written "8-9", "12-1"** are times (`hlOcrLine`); every label down the side counts on past noon in order ("7-8" under "6-7" is 19:00), a span's label stands for its middle.
- **The hours are read from label to label in straight pieces** (`hlOcrPiece`): a sheet's rows are as tall as their words, so one straight line was 10 to 20 minutes out.
- **Day headings on a slope** (`hlOcrHeads`): a photo of a screen from the side slopes one way at the top and the other at the bottom, so no turn straightens it; three or more days, each further right a later day. **A last day too small to read** (the photo's far "Fri") is added a step on when words stand there.
- **Times without am / pm or colons**: "11:30 - 1:00" ends at 13:00 (`hlOcrRange`); "1230-200" and "836- 1100" are read as a range, marked loose, and a loose start whose minutes no clock gives is taken from where the block stands (`hlOcrTimes`, `hlOcrGrid`). A time twelve hours from where its block stands is put right.
- **The block's name is its line with a course code** (`HL_CODEIN`): a corner's "Wks" before it is passed over, the words after it are the room ("Burrows Th"); a bare number beside it ("4560") is a room only when nothing else is. A code is kept in the name when what follows is only the kind of class (`HL_KINDW`: "ECON1202 - TUT", "PDTY1234 Lecture").
- **Where most blocks say their time** (a portal), a block that doesn't is a note on the page ("Permitted Clash", "Enrolled") and is left out.
- **Cells read off the picture's colours.** The shell now also hands over the picture (`ocrRead` asks `OCR_JS` again with "px": made at most 800 across, turned as read, a BMP, `ocrBmp` → 3 bytes a pixel, about half a second). For a block that doesn't say its time, its cell's top and bottom are where its colour ends (`hlPxReach`: the colour most points around its words agree on, followed from beside the words and from just past them, the farthest edge; a pale blue cell on white is only 59 out of 765 from it, so the bar is 36). In rows of an hour's span each edge goes to the nearest line between rows; otherwise a quarter hour within 4 minutes, and an end within 7 minutes of a whole half hour on is that (a calendar draws its blocks a little short; 100 minutes stays 100).
