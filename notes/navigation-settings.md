# Navigation, the footer buttons, Settings

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Navigation** (7 October 2026, the review's first critical finding):
  - **The sidebar is behind three lines** (`SideNav`, module level, `memo`, drawn through a portal on `document.body`; `.snav-root`, `.snav-burger`, `.snav`, `.snav-scrim`, `.sd-in`). It stood open at the left edge at first, with the page pushed over. The same day he asked for a menu symbol that opens it, "always closed until opened", and for all of its animations to be "smooth and clear, and seamless".
    - The three lines are fixed in the top left corner of both sides from the moment the app is entered until home (finance: hidden while the greeting and the big-year intro play). Pressed, the panel slides in over the page. It shuts on the lines again (a cross by then), on Esc (which goes no further), on a press beside it, and behind any row that is pressed. Every launch, and every return from home, starts shut. Never keep it open or remember that it was.
    - In the panel: "FLOW", the buttons on top (finance: Quick add ⌘N, then Split bill; trading: Quick log ⌘N, then Calculator), Year, Month, the parts of the month (finance: Calendar, Weekly expenses, Money flow, Goals, Debts; trading: Calendar, PnL analysis, Performance, Coins or Stocks, Breakdown on stocks), the wrapped of what is on screen, and at the foot the crocodile with how today stands.
    - **How it moves**: transitions only, on transform and opacity, so a press half-way turns everything round from where it is (checked: pressed again 180ms in, the panel goes back from the spot it had reached). The panel slides as one piece (0.54s in, 0.38s out); its lines come in one after the other, 22ms apart, once it is on its way (`--i` on `.sd-in`, a wrapper round each line so the buttons keep their own hover and press), and leave together; the page behind dims a little. The three lines become the cross by sliding to the middle and turning, never by fading over. Don't swap these for keyframes: a keyframe can't be turned round half-way.
    - Shut, the panel is parked off the left edge, painted but out of sight (no `visibility`, no `opacity: 0` on it), so the first opening has nothing to draw; its buttons are out of the Tab order meanwhile.
    - The panel is solid, with no blur behind it: the page passes under it.
    - The page keeps 64px free on each side (`body.has-side`): the left for the three lines, the right so it stays in the middle and clear of the buttons fixed in that corner. From 1408px of window up the page is exactly where it was before the rework (112px each side at 1440).
    - A part pressed from the yearly panel opens the month first (this month in the current year, else the month last looked at) and is scrolled to once it is there (`sideApi.current.go`, `SIDE_PARTS`).
    - While the panel is open the part on the line two fifths down the window is marked (`.sd-row.at`): read once a frame while the page scrolls, kept in the sidebar's own state, and not read at all while it is shut. The parts are looked up again whenever one has left the page: a card puts its content there afresh the first time it is seen and every time it is unfolded, the Debts section with it (an observer on the elements went stale for that reason).
    - Its class must stay `.snav`: `.side` is the tucked wallet, coin and globe on the selections panel.
  - **The month header is as it always was** (both sides): the pixel month on the left, "‹ Oct 2026 ›" on the right (the label goes back to the year), then the currency. The rework's first try put a year button, two arrows and the month on the left and dropped "Oct 2026"; he sent it back ("revert the month header, previous one was better"). What stays from it: **the ‹ › arrows are always on show** (`.month-nav .nav-arrow`), not only while the pointer is on the switcher. Don't rebuild this header again.
- Footer buttons (both sides), in his order: Export, Import | Settings | Add test data, Clear test data, a thin line between the groups. All are symbols without a border (`.bk-btn.ico`: upload, download, settings, science, delete_sweep), with the usual highlight under the pointer. **Export is the arrow going up and out, Import the arrow coming down and in** (11 October 2026: he found the two the wrong way round; don't swap them back). **Each says what it does in a small note above it while the pointer is on it** (`BkTip`, `.bk-tip`; same day: "have info when hovering over them. not too big ui"): its name in white and one short grey line, 11px, solid, shown 0.22s after the pointer arrives. It stands in for the system's tooltip, so these buttons have no `title`; a new footer symbol gets a `BkTip`, not a title. He asked for Export and Import; the other three were given the same so the row is one kind of thing. Undo and Reset stay words.
- **Settings** (`SettingsPop`, `SetNum`, `SetDo`, `.set-*`; 6 October 2026: he asked for the symbol and its place in the footer, not for what it holds). It opens a small pop-up with what that side could already set, through the same functions, so nothing is kept twice:
  - Finance: the currency (IDR / MYR), the daily limit of the month last shown, and "Same limit all year" (until then only a triple click on "Daily limit:").
  - Crypto: the currency shown (USDT / IDR / MYR) and the two exchange rates (until then only behind the calculator's hidden gear; emptied, a box goes back to the live rate).
  - Stocks: the broker's buy and sell fee (the stock calculator's hidden gear).
  - Every side: Appearance, the day and night switch (13 October 2026: see `notes/light-mode.md`).
  - Every side, under Appearance: Liquid glass, a slider from "More clear" to "More tinted" (14 October 2026: see "Second liquid glass pass" in `notes/style.md`). **A row of choices in the settings is a `SlideSeg`**, its grey box sliding to the choice pressed (same day: "all the buttons should slide animation if theres options").
  - Every side, last row: Developer options, On / Off (13 October 2026; it is in `SettingsPop` itself: see "Developer options").
  - While it is open it stops every key and wheel event at the window, so the panels' own handlers (arrows, Esc, a digit that opens the quick log) see none; Esc shuts it. A new setting is a `.set-row` in the side's own pop-up.
- **Settings in groups** (20 October 2026, his words: "the settings is cluttered, tidy it up"; save point `savepoints/before_settings_tidy.jsx`): `SetGroup` cards under small capital headings, as iOS's settings are, hairlines between rows, every description one short line. The side's own group first (`SettingsPop`'s `title`: Money on finance, Currency on crypto and forex, Broker fees on stocks), then **Look** (Start screen, handed in as `start`, Appearance, Liquid glass), then **App** (Automatic backup, its state as a tag on the right: `autoSaid` now returns the line and the state; Developer options). The glass slider has its own full-width line under its name, its word (Default, More clear, More tinted) beside the name. Checked with the `setlook` journey (stills on crypto and finance); the backup row only shows in the Mac app.
