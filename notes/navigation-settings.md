One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Navigation** (7 October 2026, the review's first critical finding):
  - **The sidebar is behind three lines** (`SideNav`, module level, `memo`, drawn through a portal on `document.body`; `.snav-root`, `.snav-burger`, `.snav`, `.snav-scrim`, `.sd-in`). It stood open at the left edge at first, with the page pushed over. The same day he asked for a menu symbol that opens it, "always closed until opened", and for all of its animations to be "smooth and clear, and seamless".
    - The three lines are fixed in the top left corner of both sides from the moment the app is entered until home (finance: hidden while the greeting and the big-year intro play). Pressed, the panel slides in over the page. It shuts on the lines again (a cross by then), on Esc (which goes no further), on a press beside it, and behind any row that is pressed. Every launch, and every return from home, starts shut. Never keep it open or remember that it was.
    - In the panel: "FLOW", the buttons on top (finance: Quick add ⌘N, then Split bill; trading: Quick log ⌘N, then Calculator), Year, Month, the parts of the month (finance: Calendar, Weekly expenses, Money flow, Goals, Debts; trading: Calendar, PnL analysis, Performance, Coins or Stocks, Breakdown on stocks), the wrapped of what is on screen, and at the foot the crocodile with how today stands.
    - **How it moves**: transitions only, on transform and opacity, so a press half-way turns everything round from where it is (checked: pressed again 180ms in, the panel goes back from the spot it had reached). The panel slides as one piece (0.54s in, 0.38s out); its lines come in one after the other, 22ms apart, once it is on its way (`--i` on `.sd-in`, a wrapper round each line so the buttons keep their own hover and press), and leave together; the page behind dims a little. The three lines become the cross by sliding to the middle and turning, never by fading over. Don't swap these for keyframes: a keyframe can't be turned round half-way.
    - Shut, the panel is parked off the left edge, painted but out of sight (no `visibility`, no `opacity: 0` on it), so the first opening has nothing to draw; its buttons are out of the Tab order meanwhile.
    - The panel is solid, with no blur behind it: the page passes under it.
    - The page keeps 64px free on each side (`body.has-side`): the left for the three lines, the right so it stays in the middle and clear of the buttons fixed in that corner. From 1408px of window up the page is exactly where it was before the rework (112px each side at 1440).
    - A part pressed from the yearly panel opens the month first (this month in the current year, else the month last looked at) and is scrolled to once it is there (`sideApi.current.go`, `SIDE_PARTS`).
    - While the panel is open the part on the line two fifths down the window is marked (`.sd-row.at`): read once a frame while the page scrolls, kept in the sidebar's own state, and not read at all while it is shut. The parts are looked up again whenever one has left the page: a card puts its content there afresh the first time it is seen and every time it is unfolded, the Debts section with it (an observer on the elements went stale for that reason).

## Fewer buttons: the command bar and the right-click menu (4 November 2026)
His words: "i want you to improve the ui for all of the widgets and ui. less buttons, though the core features still exists and work as intended. think outside of the box … the app should feel premium, though includes a lot of information and is easy to use".
