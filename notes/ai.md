# AI helpers and the symbols of Other

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **AI helpers** (auto-categorise, goal icons) run only in the Mac app: `AI_OK = !!window.flowAI`. In a plain browser the keyword rules work alone.
  - They are a fallback for names the keyword rules (`CAT_RULES`, `GOAL_ICON_RULES`) can't place.
  - **So a wrong answer from the keyword rules is never corrected: the AI is not asked.** That was the "sweatpants is food" he reported on 11 October 2026 ("improve the ai", without the app getting bigger): the AI had it as shopping, but the lists counted a word wherever its letters turned up, and "sweatpants" has "eat" in it (so had "sweater", "theater", "treatment"; "steam" has "tea", "business" has "bus"). Nothing about the AI was changed and nothing retrained: `ai-head.json` and the model are byte for byte what they were, so the app is no bigger.
    - **How a list word counts now** (`inferCat`, `CAT_WORDS`): as a word of its own (a plural s allowed); from 4 letters also at the start of a longer word ("kopi" in "kopiko"); from 5 also inside one ("pants" in "sweatpants"). Written with a space after it in its list ("es ", "train ", "water "), only as a word of its own. Written with = before it ("=tiket", "=sewa", "=wifi", "=gas", "=shopee": 22 of them), only when it is the whole note: beside another word these meant too many things (tiket bioskop, sewa mobil, router wifi) and the AI reads those better. When several words fit, a word of its own beats a start beats an inside, then the longer word wins ("shopee food" over "shopee", "grabfood" over "grab").
    - Answers are kept per note (`CAT_SEEN`): it is asked for every entry on every drawing.
    - Clothes were not in the shopping list at all (pants, jeans, hoodie, sweater, socks and so on are now), nor the food deliveries (grabfood, gofood, shopee food).
    - Measured on every labelled note of `tools/ai` (9,222): of the notes the lists placed, 470 of 2,425 were wrong before (19.4%), 88 of 1,990 after (4.4%). On the 339 notes of `tests.json`, lists and AI together: 309 right before, 335 after, none that was right now wrong. (The shipped head may have seen some of those notes, so 335 flatters the AI; the comparison holds, it is the same head both times.)
    - What is still wrong is mostly a food word in a thing ("periuk nasi", "meja kopi", "termos es"). To fix one, add the whole phrase to its list: the longer match wins.
    - In a plain browser there is no AI, so a note the lists now pass on ("tiket bioskop", "wifi rumah") has no category there.
  - **14 October 2026, "test the auto categorize AI, find AI flaws and fix them, no shortcuts"**: `tools/ai/probe.mjs` (221 hard notes run through the app's own path: the lists, then the model and `ai-head.json`; it prints every miss). Of the lists' wrong answers over all 9,007 labelled notes, 86 of 1,896 placed, the patterns were fixed and **13 are left, with none that was right changed** (checked against the save point `before_cat_fixes.jsx`; the held-out notes and the probe: none worse):
    - **A food word on a thing is the thing** (`CAT_THINGS`: meja kopi, sendok makan, termos es, grinder kopi, steak knife, snack kucing → shopping), unless it is a dish or a place (`CAT_DISHES`: nasi kucing, soto saring, sate kelinci, martabak kertas, gang kelinci). A plate and a bowl count only as words of their own ("nasi sepiring" is food).
    - **A thing with a service done to it is the service** (`CAT_SERVICES`: jahit, permak, reparasi, repair, sablon, cuci baju/sepatu, kursus → other), unless it names what the service is done with (`CAT_SUPPLIES`: sabun cuci baju stays shopping).
    - "nasi", "sate", "bill", "lego" only as words of their own (nasional, satellite, billiard, legoland); "e money " likewise (gav**e money**). A small `other` list in `CAT_RULES` (couriers, steam wallet, makeup artist, hospital, vet bill, arisan); airlines, uber eats, Malay car loans and insurance, fines, a water bill, paylater added where they belong.
    - Tried and taken back: "book"/"buku" as shopping (fixed one note, broke four: book club, book signing).
    - With the model, the probe is 220/221 (the one left: "eat pray love book"). "sweatpants" was right already in his installed app (the 11 October fix is in it).
    - **Size** (he asked to compress without losing anything): every smaller build of the model scored worse, so it stays; see `notes/handoff.md`.
  - Fully offline: a small embedding model in a separate process, loaded on first use and closed after five idle minutes (the Mac has 8 GB RAM; the helper uses about 110 MB).
  - How it decides: the model turns the note into a vector; a linear classifier plus a vote of the 5 most similar known notes picks the label.
  - Answers are remembered in `ai-cache.json` in the app's data folder; the cache empties itself whenever `ai-head.json` changes. "Changes" is read from what is in the file (its size and the start of it), not from its date: the installed app's copy has a date of its own, so opening it and `npm run app` in turn used to empty the answers and run the helper's setup again every time.
  - Held-out score (notes the keyword rules miss, not seen in training): expenses 197/207 (95%), goal icons 38/40 (95%). The 2.5 GB Gemma 3 4B it replaced scored 188/207 and 38/40 on the same notes.
  - Its weak spot is a rare single word that is in none of the lists; the fix is to add that word to `cats.txt` and retrain.
  - Dead ends, don't retry: zero-shot embeddings without training (65%), 1–1.5B chat models (53–76%), `getLlama({ gpu: false })` (no CPU-only binary on Apple Silicon; it tries to compile from source and bloats `node_modules`).
- **The symbols of Other** (`OTHER_SYMS`, `ICON_CHOICES`, `SYM_NAME`, `inferSym`, `wordPick`, `listWords`, `symOf`, `aiSyms`; 13 October 2026: "change these symbols to actual symbols that i would use (that is out of the top 5 category)", "build a small ai (lower performance model) for each of those symbols that auto picks the category"). The picker under Other held 34 symbols, 24 of them of the four named categories (forks, cars, bags, bills), which an expense in Other never is.
  - **36 symbols, six rows of six, each a kind of spending that lands in Other**: doctor, medicine, dentist, haircut, massage and beauty, gym / sports, games, movies, concerts and events, outings, trips / hotel, gifts, weddings and parties, charity and zakat, family, dates / kids, pets, lent to someone, laundry and tailor, repairs, home help / shipping, printing, documents and permits, courses, books, work / bank cash and top-ups, savings, investing, fines, cigarettes and vape, anything else (`apps`, the plain one). Each says its name under the pointer, and the one in force is lit. **Which kinds is my choice** (he said "symbols that i would use"), taken from what the labelled notes in `tools/ai` hold under "other"; to change one, edit its line in `OTHER_SYMS` and its header in `syms.txt`, then retrain.
  - **A symbol is picked by itself** for an expense that is in Other (by hand or by the AI) or in no category yet, unless he picked one: first by the words of its line in `OTHER_SYMS` (read exactly as the category lists are, by the same function, `wordPick`; a space after a word: only as a word of its own, = before it: only as the whole note), then by the AI helper (`window.flowAI.pick("sym", …)`, asked once per expense of the open day). It shows where the hand-picked one does (the row's reel, the weekly chart's opened note), in the same gold.
  - **It decides a symbol, never a category**: `inferCat` and every total are untouched (the category lists read 10,863 labelled notes exactly as before the change). So "haircut" has its scissors at once but is only counted under Other once he or the AI says so, as before.
  - **The small AI is a third head on the model that is already there** (no second model, nothing more in memory, 14ms a note; `ai-head.json` grew by 2.2 MB, so does the app). A linear layer and the five most similar known notes, as the other two. **An answer it is not sure enough of is no symbol** (`knn.floor` 0.55 and `knn.none` "apps" in the head; `classify` in `electron/ai-classify.mjs`; the other two heads have no floor and answer as before): a wrong symbol is worse than the plain one. How alike the nearest known note is was tried as the measure first and tells nothing (0.70 to 0.97 for sense and nonsense alike): don't go back to it.
  - Measured on 156 held-out notes written to be hard (slang, brands, titles; `symsA` in `tests.json`): the words place 27, 25 right; of the 129 that reach the AI 93 are right, 29 are left plain and 7 get a wrong symbol (without the floor: 104 right, 24 wrong). Of 16 notes that name nothing ("bayar pak joko", "asdf"; `symsNone`) 15 are left plain. On its own 1,779 training notes the words place 1,210, 1,195 right. `npm run train-ai` prints all of it.
  - **The order of the lists only settles a tie** (`- k.order / 10` in `wordPick`): as a whole number, with 36 lists it outweighed three letters of a word. For the four category lists nothing changes.
  - The weak spot is a rare word in no list (slang: "sebat", "garpit"; a title: "atomic habits"): it is left plain or, now and then, wrong. The cure is the word in its line of `OTHER_SYMS` (no retraining) or the note in `syms.txt` (retrain).
  - A symbol saved from the old picker (a taxi, an ice cream) is still drawn as it was.
  - Checked with the `others` journey (`--shots --fresh`; the hidden shell has no AI helper, so the words alone): "potong rambut" with no category the scissors, "obat flu" in Other the pill, "sesuatu" the plain one, "kopi" the fork, a hand-picked one as it was, "futsal sama temen" the ball; the picker's 36 with their names, every one drawn at its own width (no name the icon font lacks), the pill lit; Gym picked there was saved and shown. The trained head itself was asked from Node as the helper asks it, about thirteen notes it had never seen. **Not seen: the helper answering inside the Mac app** (the same call as the category's, with "sym" added to `AI_KINDS` in `electron/main.cjs`).
- **The model made smaller, giving the same** (22 October 2026, his words: "condense the app so it doesnt take too much space in my computer when downloaded"). Of the model's 322 MB, 214 MB was its word table (262,144 pieces × 768 numbers), most of it pieces of scripts no note is written in. `tools/ai/prune-vocab.mjs` keeps every piece made only of Latin-1, general punctuation, currency, symbols, arrows, the tokenizer's space mark (▁) and emoji, plus the bytes and the nine real control pieces, in their order (pad, eos, bos, unk keep ids 0–3): 155,101 pieces, 328.6 MB → 238.4 MB. The tokenizer only cuts a text into pieces that are in it, so a note of those characters is cut exactly as before.
  - Proved: `tools/ai/check-prune.mjs <full> <pruned>` on all 10,817 notes of the training and test files plus odd ones: 10,814 cut the same, none of the kept characters cut differently, the numbers for 316 notes differ by exactly 0; only notes in Japanese, Arabic and Thai script differ (they are read less well now). And `OUT=<scratch> node tools/ai/train.mjs` on the pruned model scored exactly as before (expenses 217/227, goals 38/40, symbols 93/129, none 15/16) and wrote a head **byte for byte the same** as `electron/ai-head.json`.
  - The full model is `tools/ai/models/flow-ai-full.gguf` (ignored by git, not shipped), the ids kept in `tools/ai/models/flow-ai.keep.json`. To make it again from a new full model: `node tools/ai/prune-vocab.mjs tools/ai/models/flow-ai-full.gguf models/flow-ai.gguf`, then the check.
  - With it, the package leaves out the library's source maps (`!**/node_modules/**/*.map`, 6 MB) and the DMG is bzip2 (`dmg.format: "UDBZ"`; this electron-builder offers no LZMA). DMG 445 → 357 MB, installed 603 → 527 MB. What is left: Electron itself 237 MB (English only already), the model 238 MB, the AI library 24 MB, the app 7 MB. Not done, as each changes what the AI gives: fewer bits per number (measured 15 October, every smaller build scored worse), fewer dimensions for the stored notes.
- **The heads' stored notes as raw bytes** (23 October 2026, "shrink the app size further" "without compromising for performance"): `ai-head.json` kept the 9,222 + 940 + 1,935 example vectors as base64 text, a third bigger than their bytes and decoded at every start. They are now `electron/ai-head.bin` (9.3 MB) beside a 0.5 MB `ai-head.json` whose `knn.bin` says where each head's bytes are; `loadHeads` / `saveHeads` in `ai-classify.mjs` read and write the pair for `ai.mjs`, `train.mjs` (also `ONLY=sym`) and `probe.mjs`; `unpack` still takes base64 too. Proved: every head unpacked from the old file and from the new pair the same to the byte (vectors, their lengths, everything else), and `probe.mjs` 220/221 as before. The shell's cache fingerprint is taken from the JSON, so the remembered AI answers are asked afresh once after this.
  - Sizes as Finder counts them (decimal MB): installer 445 → 357 MB, installed 632 → 549 MB (Electron 248, the model 238, the AI library 25, the heads 10, the app 8).
- **"pack" is Other, with the cigarettes symbol** (23 October 2026, his words: "whenever i input 'pack' its another word for cigarette, so just list it as others"): `"pack "` and `"packs "` (a word of its own: "pack", "2 pack", "pack sampoerna", not "backpack") in `CAT_RULES.other` and in the Cigarettes & vape symbol's words (`OTHER_SYMS`). The keyword lists come before the AI, so earlier "pack" entries follow too, unless their category was picked by hand.
