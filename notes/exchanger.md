# The money exchanger

Asked for on 20 October 2026: "when double clicking the switching currency button, i want to add a money exchanger ui. make sure the prices are accurate. choosing the currency: 3 currencies that are recently used and other currencies that are listed as A-Z. make it so i can search. live prices updated from the internet. make me be able to input from one currency and the other one, not just 1"; then "I want all the text and inputs to have an animation when switching between currencies, and have an option to add other currencies", "Make sure the switch currency animation has no lag and runs smoothly", and "make a shortcut for the money exchanger".

## Ways in
- A **fast** double click on the currency pill: finance's IDR / MYR pill, the trading sides' pill and the position calculator's (`onCurClick`). Fast means the second click within `CUR_TRIPLE_MS` (300ms) of the first, timed by the app itself (20 October 2026: with the Mac's own double click, `e.detail` / `onDoubleClick`, two clicks a second apart with the pointer still opened it, and he wants a pause between clicks to switch). On a trading side three quick clicks still mean MYR, so the exchanger opens `CUR_TRIPLE_MS` after the second click if no third came. Both clicks switch as usual, so the pill ends where it was.
- The IDX side's IDR tag, which switches nothing: a fast double click opens it.
- ⌘E anywhere in an app (finance in `PnlCalendar`, a trading side in `TradeTracker`); ⌘E again closes it.
- Home's Exchange widget (below), its ↗; Home's Shortcuts widget: a fifth button, Exchange (`DG_ACTS`, `go("fx")`). Five buttons sit in one row when the widget is wide, one column when it is tall, else two by two with the fifth across both.

## What it is (`Exchanger`, in `src/App.jsx` above `HomeDash`)
- Rows of currencies, two to six. Typing in any row converts all the others; the row typed in is lit. The arrow on the seam between the first two rows swaps their currencies (the amount stays in the row typed in: 100 USD → IDR becomes 100 IDR → USD). Each row says what one of its currency is in the typed one ("1 MYR = 4.408,92 IDR"), with "· daily" when one of the two has no live rate.
- A row's currency is changed from its badge and name: the list slides in over the rows. A currency already in another row trades places with it ("Swaps"). Add currency appends a row (one already there is shown "Added" and can't be picked). A row's ✕ shows under the pointer when there are more than two.
- The list: a search (code, name, sign), then Recent (the last three picked) and All currencies, A–Z by code with a letter above each group. Arrows and Enter pick, Esc goes back, Esc again closes.
- Figures are written the Indonesian way, as everywhere in the app: dots between thousands, a decimal comma. A "." typed is taken as the comma; the field puts the dots in itself and keeps the caret among the digits. Pasted text is read as written ("1,234.56" and "1.234,56" are both 1234,56). A row shows its currency's own decimals (`FX_DP0`, `FX_DP3`); under 1, enough for three figures that count.
- While it is open every key is its own (`useSheet`'s keys, at the window, stop the rest): the calculator under it would take Enter and ⌘Z, the panels the arrows.
- Kept: the rows, which one was typed in, the amount and the recent three under `FX_EX_KEY` ("flow-exchange", through `store`, so per account; deleted with the account in `userErase`). It opens as it was left, with the currency it was opened from added on top if missing.

- **The sheet is as tall as its rows** (20 October 2026, the fine-tuning: at a fixed 600px three rows left a band of empty glass), between 440px (room for the list's search and a handful of currencies) and the window. It hangs from a fixed top (`max(40px, 11vh)`), so a row added or removed moves only what is under it: the rows below, Add currency and the foot glide (FLIP).

## Home's Exchange widget (`DgFx`; `xch` on the trading sides, `fxch` on finance)
- Asked for on 20 October 2026: "this money exchanger ui should have a widget in the home dashboard". Added from Edit → Add widget; Small, Medium, Large.
- It is the exchanger's own rows and amount: typing in it is typing in the exchanger (it opens showing what was typed on Home), and what is typed in the exchanger shows on Home when it closes. Both go through one store (`fxMem`, `fxPrefsPut`, `fxRatesPut`, `fxRefresh`, `useFx`): the sheet hears the rates, the widget the rates and the rows.
- Small: the first two currencies as one rate in the pixel type, the way round that reads above 1 ("14.079,5 · IDR for 1 SGD"), counting when it changes; a press opens the exchanger. Medium: the first two rows to type in (a row's name from 330px). Large: as many rows as fit, each with what one of it is in the row typed in. Its head has the swap of the first two (rolling as in the sheet) and ↗ to the exchanger. The foot: "Live · 21:43", or when the rates are from.
- Its rates are asked when it comes on screen and once a minute while Home shows it (not while the window is hidden), like the Live prices widget; none in the gallery's preview.

## The rates (`electron/main.cjs`: `fetchFxTable`, `fetchFxLive`)
- Every currency against the US dollar from ExchangeRate-API's daily table (`open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD`, 166 currencies; it is already the fallback of `fetchRates`). Kept an hour in the shell. This is also the list the picker offers.
- The currencies on screen live: TradingView's forex table (`FX_IDC:USDxxx` and `FX_IDC:xxxUSD`, ICE's mid-market quotes; of the two the one written with more digits). What it does not give, from Yahoo (`xxx=X`, one request a currency). A source that fails rests ten minutes, like the price sources. Asked when it opens, when a currency is put on screen (not again within 15s), and every minute while open.
  - On 20 October 2026 `scanner.tradingview.com` stopped resolving on his line for a while (it had answered half an hour before): Yahoo was added for that.
  - Live and daily differed by 0.02–0.33% that day (USD/IDR 17,995 live, 18,055 daily).
- USDT: tether's own price in rupiah from `fetchRates` (CoinGecko, the crypto side's), so USDT is worked out through IDR. Until that arrives USDT counts as one dollar.
- Rejected: CoinGecko's `exchange_rates` table. Every currency there comes from bitcoin's price in it, which carries that market's premium, a percent or two off.
- A live rate more than a fifth away from the daily one is taken for a bad tick and left out.
- The last rates are kept under `FX_RATES_KEY` ("flow-fx-rates", `rawStore`: nobody's data, no figure of his is in it), so offline it shows what it had, and says from when. In the browser (no shell) it has the built-in USDT rates only.
- The status under the title: "Live · updated 20:40" when every row is live; "Daily rate for … · the rest live"; "Offline · rates from …"; "Getting the latest rates…". The foot says the rates are mid-market: banks and money changers add their margin.

## Motion (all transform and opacity)
- A currency switched (swap, pick): every text that changes rolls like a counter's wheel (`FxRoll`): the old line and the new one move together by one whole line, cut off above and below only (`clip-path: inset(0 -400px)`), so they never stand on top of each other (first tried as a 0.6em fade: mid-way the two texts sat on each other). The badge shrinks away as the new one grows in. The swap's second row rolls the other way, so the two read as trading places; rows follow one another by 30ms. A change made by typing is written in place (`turn` tells the two apart). The arrow turns half a turn per swap.
- The list slides in from the right as the rows slide a little left and fade (both panes have their own layer, `will-change: transform`). It is drawn the first time it is wanted and kept; its names are worked out in a quiet moment after opening; its rows skip layout off screen (`content-visibility`).
- Rows come in one after another on opening; a row added rises in; a row removed shrinks away and the ones under it glide up (FLIP, `el.animate` on transform).
- Reduce motion: fades only.
- Lag: the currency signs are a fixed table (`FX_SYM`): working them out with `Intl.NumberFormat` took 36ms for the list. Signs Inter has no letter for (؋ ֏ ৳ ៛ ฿) are left out, as the system's font for them would be looked up the first time (30–50ms).

## The calculator beside it (`CalcPad`, just above `Exchanger`; 23 October 2026)
- His words: "make me a calculator, this is right beside the exchanger and uses the same shortcut", then "a precise, easy to use calculator that is user friendly though enough to calculate expenses, incomes, etc. just a simple calculator". Save point from before it: `savepoints/before_calc_beside_fx.jsx`.
- Read as: it opens and closes with the exchanger, every way in (⌘E, the pill's double click, Home), standing to its right (16px apart, tops level; 468 + 320px). Esc, ⌘E or a press outside close both. If he meant a tab or a button inside the exchanger instead, that is the place to change.
- Keys: AC, ⌫ (the last digit; after a sign, back into the number before it), % (after + or − it is of what stands before it: 50.000 + 10% adds 5.000; otherwise ÷ 100), ÷ × − +, **000** (rupiah are typed in thousands), 0, the decimal comma, =. × and ÷ before + and − (2 + 3 × 4 = 14). A second sign replaces the first; a sign after = goes on from the result. The sign waiting for its number is lit.
- The display: the sum as typed above (the last 37 characters when long), the number being typed or the result, and under it the total so far ("= 475.000") while a sum is being typed.
- **Exact for money**: every step is rounded to 15 significant figures (`calcClean`), so 0,1 + 0,2 is 0,3 and rupiah up to `MAX_AMOUNT` come out to the last digit (15 digits a number, typed). Dividing by 0 says "Can't divide by 0"; past 15 figures "Too big to show exactly". Figures the Indonesian way. Checked in node: 17 cases, all right.
- The keyboard: the exchanger's key handler hands a key to the calculator when the focus is inside it (its keypad pressed, or the pad clicked): digits, , and . (both the comma), + - * x / :, %, Enter or =, Backspace, Delete or C. The key pressed dips on screen (`el.animate`, transform only). The status line says "Your keys come here" with a green dot while it has them. Typing in the exchanger's rows stays theirs.
- What was on it is kept until the app closes (`calcMem`), not saved.
- Checked with the `calcpad` journey (`--shots`, the usual profile): where the two stand, 150.000 × 3 + 25.000 = 475.000 pressed, 0,1 + 0,2 = 0,3 and 50.000 + 10% typed, ⌘E closing both, the exchanger's amount untouched, no console error.
