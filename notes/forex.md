# Forex

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`. This is a part of the trading tracker (`TradeTracker`): read `notes/trading.md` with it.

- **Forex** (8 October 2026: "make a forex app beside the idx on the very right. just copy all of the crypto apps but change it to forex terms, change logged trades input boxes to match the metatrader 5 app"). `MARKETS.fx`, told to the tracker as `market="fx"`:
  - It is the crypto side in a dollar account. `base` stays `"USDT"` (the dollar every amount and rate is worked in, so nothing in the currency code had to change) and `unit: "USD"` is what it is called on screen: the pill, the settings, the calculator, the tooltips. USD ⇄ IDR ⇄ MYR works as on crypto, with the same rates.
  - Kept under `pnl-fx-usd` and `pnl-fx-prefs`, exported as `<first name>-fx-export-DD-MM-YYYY.json` into `forex exports/` (`EXPORT_SIDES` in `electron/main.cjs`), `app: "flow-fx"`; it refuses the other sides' files. Its pictures' ids begin `fx-` (`M.pics`; `shotSweep(trades, pre)` now takes the side's own start, crypto's is "").
  - Words: Symbol for Coin, **Buy / Sell** for Long / Short (`M.sideName`; kept on the trade as `side: "long" | "short"` like crypto), Pairs for Coins, no grey text after the name.
  - **The journal reads like a position in MetaTrader 5** (`fx` on `TradeSheet`, `TradeView`, `TradeJournal`): Win / Breakeven / Loss and **Profit** in $ on top, then Symbol, Type (Buy / Sell), Volume (lots, two decimals), Time frame, Duration, R / Factor; and Open price, S / L, T / P, Close price (prices, up to six decimals), Swap, Commission. Setup, Review, Pictures and the Deposits line as on crypto. Full TP %, Risk % and Leverage are not on it.
    - **Profit is what MetaTrader shows as Profit; the trade's `pnl` is Profit + Swap + Commission**, as the balance in MetaTrader moves (`sheetTrade`, `fxGross`). Swap is typed with a minus when it was charged; Commission is always a cost, whatever sign is typed. The form says what it comes to ("Net result") and the row and every total use that. So a trade can be a Win in the form and a loss on its row when the costs are bigger than the profit.
    - **Pips are worked out, never typed** (`fxPip`, `fxPips`): from the open and the close price, and only for a pair of two currencies in `FX_CCY` (a pip is 0.01 where the yen is quoted, 0.0001 otherwise). Gold, silver, indices and anything else have no pip everybody agrees on, so none is shown. Don't guess one.
    - Kept on the trade when filled in (`journalOf`): `vol`, `open`, `close`, `sl`, `tpp`, `swap`, `comm` (the stocks side's `lots`, `entry`, `exit` are whole numbers, so forex has its own).
    - The row: `Win  EURUSD  [Buy] [1h]  +2R  0.10 LOT  +27.0 PIPS  5H 40M  the setup…  +$25.85`. The view: Open, Close with the pips beside it, S / L, T / P, and Profit, Swap, Commission when there is a swap or a commission.
  - **Calculator** (`PositionCalc` told `fxm`; the maths is `calcForex`, `fxSpec`): the crypto pop-up sized in lots. Symbol, Buy / Sell (read from the levels or locked), entry, stop loss, Risk $, the account's leverage (only for the margin; it may be left empty), partial TPs and a full TP.
    - The volume is the largest that loses no more than the risk at the stop, in steps of 0.01 lot. A lot is 100,000 of the base currency, 100 ounces of gold (XAU), 5,000 of silver (XAG). **For any other symbol he types the contract size** ("1 lot = … units"): it differs from broker to broker and is never guessed.
    - A pair's profit is made in its quote currency and turned into dollars as MetaTrader does: quoted in dollars as it is; dollar-based (USDJPY) divided by the price it is closed at; **a cross (EURJPY, EURGBP) through the dollar rate of its quote currency, which he types** in a box that appears for it (USDJPY, GBPUSD).
    - Partial targets close whole hundredths of a lot (one that comes to less than 0.01 closes nothing, and a note says so); the last target closes what is left. Tiles: Volume, Margin, Position size, Per pip (Units where there is no pip). The Move column is in pips for a currency pair.
    - **No forex price is fetched** (`quoteBook.can` is false on this side): the only requests the app makes are the two in `electron/main.cjs`, and forex is neither. The entry is typed.
    - Tests: `node tools/fx-calc-test.mjs` (64 checks): what a symbol is, six positions and two partial plans worked out by hand, 400 positions against an independent "broker", what is refused, 30,000 random positions (never over the risk, never a hundredth of a lot too small). Run it after touching `calcForex` or `calcPosition`: the forex maths uses calcPosition's checks, and its block sits inside the one `tools/calc-test.mjs` reads.
  - Test data (`TEST_PAIRS`, `testBoxes(journal, pnl, pair, side)`, `TEST_SETUPS_FX`, `FX_TEST_PX`): a volume, prices, a swap and a commission that add up to the trade's result, in the direction of its side.
  - **The forex die** (`FxDie`, `FX_DIE`, `.fx-die`, `.fxd-f`, `.fxd-core`; 11 October 2026: "change the forex symbol, get creative, dont reuse coins. showcase the 3 big currencies for the symbol"): an ivory cube seen corner on, so three faces show at once: the dollar on top (green), the euro on the left (blue), the yen on the right (red), each pressed into its face. The faces behind carry the same three. With the coin (a disc) and the globe (a ball) the trading panel is three plain solids in a row.
    - Until then it was three coins (`FxCoins`: a platinum dollar, a gold euro, a copper yen, each the Bitcoin coin's own build, made smaller with `zoom`). All of that is gone, with its metals (`coinMetal`, `FX_PLAT`, `FX_COPPER`, `COIN_PLAT`, `COIN_COPPER`). Don't go back to coins for forex.
    - It stands in the coin's own boxes (`.coin-3d > .coin-s2 > .coin-s3`, then `.fx-die`, which holds the corner-on pose), so the pointer's tilt, the float, the ground shadow, the tucking aside and the toss are the coin's, untouched: entering forex rolls the die three whole turns and it lands in the same pose. The unit is still `.coin-unit.fx` and its motion figures `fxRig` (the lustre and the pointer's light have nothing to write to now, which `writeCoin` allows).
    - Twelve flat pieces: six faces (184px, round corners, a layer each) and six of a core 6px inside them in the shade's colour, which fills the corners the round corners leave open. The light is painted on the faces once, for the resting pose (top brightest, left in half shade, right in shade). The symbol on top is turned 45 degrees so it stands upright in the diamond the top is seen as.
    - While it floats it rocks 7 degrees either way about its upright (`fxRock`, a transform keyframe); under the pointer and tucked away it holds still, like the float.
    - Checked with `tools/perf` journey `fxdie` (stills: tucked beside the globe, in focus, under the pointer, in mid-toss).
  - **The trading panel is a row of three** (`MARKET_ROW`, `marketFocus` is `"crypto"`, `"idx"` or `"fx"`, `stepMarket`): the one in the middle is in focus, its neighbours are tucked to the left and the right, and the one two places away is out of sight beyond its neighbour (`.side.far`, as a far wallet is). The wheel goes one along and stops at the ends. The forex die is first drawn 0.9s after the trading panel has opened, out of sight (a delayed `visibility` on `.coin-unit.fx.side.far`), so it is not painted in the middle of that move.
