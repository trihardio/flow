- **Projection** (`projectMonth`, `renderProjection`, `.proj-*`): a card in Money flow between the efficiency score and the insights, for the current month only: how much will be left on the last day.
  - What it shows (reworked 5 October 2026; he asked for a new look, a backtest and more accuracy): the amount (or how much short), the likely range, then the month as a path (`ProjChart`): spent so far as a solid line, the expected rest dashed, the range as a cone opening from today, income as a dashed ceiling with what is left marked at the end, bills due as dots on their day. Under it tiles (weekday, weekend, room a day, bills due, big buys, already entered, income due) and its track record: the projection worked out again as of this day in each of the last three months, against how they really ended (`projRecord`).
  - How it reckons, three kinds of spending apart: everyday spending per day of the week from the last 12 weeks, recent days counting more; bills that come back monthly and aren't paid yet; big one-off buys read from the whole year behind (`PROJ_LONG`), counted per month: how many are still to come follows from how many each month behind had, how many are made already and how much of the month is gone (`projBigLeft`; with fewer than `PROJ_MONTHS` months behind, so many a day by chance). `path` is the running total day by day, which the chart draws.
  - Later days that already hold entries are taken as written; only the empty days are projected ("Stop at today" in the developer options leaves them empty).
  - **It counts the planned expenses** (`plans` handed to `projectMonth`, `planSum`, `planDue`, `planBig`, `bigKeep`; 12 October 2026: "improve the projection on money flow, backtest 3-4 data and make sure its accurate"). A plan for today or a later day of this month is taken as it stands, on its day, on top of what that day would cost anyway; a tile "Planned" says so. A plan that is itself a big buy is one of the big buys expected, not one more (the number still to come is lowered by it); a plan named as a bill that is due stands in for that bill. With nothing planned every figure is exactly what it was.
    - **What the backtest says, and it is not much**: with half of the big buys that really came later in the month given as plans, off by (with → without): balanced 14.2 → 14.1%, erratic 16.9 → 17.4, big spender 10.4 → 10.5, real-life months 10.3 → 11.0 (within 10% of the real end 63% of the time, was 60%); the range still holds the real end 70–77% of the time. Big buys that come by chance are no easier to count for knowing some of them: the ones not planned are as uncertain as before. The gain is where his months are regular, and in the plain fact that a 150.000 haircut he has entered is in the figure. He was told this in those words.
    - The everyday pattern, the big-buy count and the range were **not** retuned (the list below still holds; the projection is near its floor).
  - **The headline follows the range** (`cross` in `renderProjection`; 12 October 2026: "561Rb short" stood big and red beside "likely −1,52jt to 399,5Rb" and a record of 63% off: "the loudest thing on the card is the least certain").

- **The monthly spending rule** (`RULE_FAMS`, `ruleFrom`, `cleanRule`, `ruleStand`, `ruleDaily`, `RulePop`, `ruleNow`, `renderRule`, `limitOf`, `.rule-*`; 15 October 2026, his words: "a monthly spending rule … something like 70/30 rule, 70% spend and 30% save or the other way around", "find rules like this and others"). He chose: it tracks the month **and sets the daily limit**; all four families; a section in Money flow **and** a line in the month's card; one rule for every month, any month allowed its own.
  - Families and presets: Spend / save (70/30, 30/70, 80/20, his own split); Needs / wants / save (50/30/20, 60/20/20; Food, Transport, Bills are needs, Shopping and Other wants, each movable); Living / save / give (70/20/10, its 10% is giving, fed by Other's Charity & zakat, Gifts, Weddings & parties and Family symbols: my reading, a family of its own); 40/30/20/10 (daily needs, obligations = Bills, saving, social = those symbols); the 60% Solution (committed, irregular = Other's Trips, Hotel, Repairs, Doctor, Dentist, fun, long-term saving, retirement). A rule is saved with the entries (`rule`, `ruleByMonth`, false = no rule that month), goes into backups and is replaced by an import.
