# Warm-up and first launch

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Warm-up** (`warm*`, near the top of `App.jsx`; 3 October 2026): Rassam's complaint was that everything lags the first time it is opened after a launch (the wallets, a card scrolled to, a chart) and never the second time.
  - What a first showing pays for: text shaped in each font, weight and size; the system asked for a font for the characters Inter lacks (▾ ✕ ▮ ▬ → ← ⇄ ↔ ≈ ★: 30–50ms the first time); pictures decoded; and a small graphics program built for each new kind of paint, blur and blend (a burst of 25–40 of those froze a few frames). None of it can be asked for ahead of time except by drawing the thing.
  - So each screen is **filed** a moment after it settles (`useWarmScreen(key)`, in both `PnlCalendar` and `TradeTracker`): its markup goes into IndexedDB `flow-warm` (apart from his data, never in a backup), in idle gaps (`warmWhenIdle`), again at 7s and 25s if he is still there. Of a pop-up (`…+chart`, `+recap`, `+cat`, `+calc`) only the pop-up is filed. The 3D coin and the stage's backdrop are left out (`data-nowarm`).
  - At the next launch `warmReplay` puts each filed screen back for two frames (plus one per extra window-height, `WARM_MAX_SLICES`) in `.warm-host`: `inert`, `opacity: 0.004`, animations off, the hidden cards shown. The second frame repaints every second element (`.touch`), because redrawing part of a tile has programs of its own. Wallets and trading selection first, then panels, then pop-ups, most recently used first; as many as fit while the opening stands still.
  - **What does not fit is drawn afterwards, in quiet moments** (`warmRest`, `warmQueue`, `WARM_QUIET_MS`, `WARM_PRESS_MS`, `WARM_PIECE`, `WARM_SLOT_MS`, `.wt`; 9 October 2026: he asked for all the first-open lag to go). With four sides there are 24 filed screens and only 8 to 14 fit under the opening; the pop-ups come last and never had their turn, so every chart, calculator and wrapped was drawn for the first time when he pressed it.
    - **Only while he is doing nothing**: not until the pointer, the wheel and the keys have been still for 0.7s (2.4s after a press or a key), not while the app says something is on its way in or out (`warm.hold`, written by `PnlCalendar` on every render: the opening, the login, the way into an app and its greeting) nor for 1.4s after that is over (`warm.until`, counted from the render that ends it: read only when the work looks, the end was missed and the drawing began 0.4s after a panel appeared), not while a number is counting (`warm.moved`, written by `useCountUp`), not while the window is hidden, and to begin the page has to be at rest (a long idle gap).
    - **A piece at a time, never a screen at a go**: a screen is read into a `template`, then put into the box 120 elements at a time and laid out there and then (`draw`, a generator: each `yield` is a piece), about 5ms of work, then a frame; then the touch, a piece at a time (`.wt`), then the lower window-heights. An idle-time version that drew a whole screen at once (20 to 170ms) had been tried and dropped because it could land on a press: don't go back to whole screens.
    - **The first movement of the pointer hides the box at once** (`opacity: 0` on the one element, so nothing of it is drawn while he is at work) and the work stops where it is. A window resized gives up the half-built screen.
    - The screens of the side he is on go first (`warm.at`, `warmSide`); one he has opened himself meanwhile is left out (`warm.seen`). After a change of account (no opening) all of them wait for a quiet moment.
    - While a screen is in the box the page holds a second copy of its cards, so **anything that looks an element up across the whole page would find the copy**: the sidebar's three look-ups go through `warm.page` for that reason. Keep new look-ups inside the page (`warm.page.querySelector`), never `document.querySelector`.
    - Measured, traced (9 October 2026, the `rested` journey): the 15 screens left over were drawn in 1.6s of quiet; the page was never busy for 12ms in one go (two pieces of 10 and 11ms, the rest under 8), the graphics process never for 10ms. First openings after it, without → with: calculator 154 → 40–80ms busy (longest 103 → 28–37), wrapped 138 → 20–25ms (graphics process 147 → 12–23ms), finance chart 158 → 91–137ms; a finance month that was not the side used last: layout 30 → 20ms, the wait for its first picture 49 → 13ms. Single runs differ a lot (see `tools/perf`), the calculator and the wrapped were better in every run. **What it can't reach**: a screen's own script still runs for the first time when he opens it (on a finance month about 20ms more than the second time), and a month is heavy every time it opens (about 1500 elements: 70ms in one go, traced, with everything warm).
  - **What the warm-up threw away is cleared while the opening still stands** (`window.gc()` at the end of `warmReplay`; the shell passes `--expose-gc` on every launch since 9 October 2026, it used to on a first launch only). Left to the engine it was cleared during the first presses: 17ms as the wallet came back into focus, 33ms as the first month opened.
    - **Tried and of no use, don't retry**: giving the script's memory more room at start (`--initial-old-space-size=128`: one clean-up of 67ms instead of five small ones; with `--min-semi-space-size=16` a 16ms one in the middle of the month's opening).
  - Stills taken during it differ from stills without it by nothing: at 0.004 it rounds to no change.
  - It never touches the real panels or their state. A screen he has never opened is not warmed; a new pop-up or panel gets warmed by giving it a key in the `useWarmScreen` call.
  - Measured in the off-screen engine (first opening after a launch, before → after): finance month 77 → 40ms busy (second opening 25), chart 55 → 21, wrapped 27 → 16, crypto calculator 83 → 18, crypto chart 35 → 11; graphics programs built after the start 106 → about 40 (finance), 70 → about 30 (trading). What is left is the script's own first run.
  - Of a pop-up on the trading sides, what is filed is the pop-up inside an empty copy of the `.term` wrapper. From the day that wrapper went in (6 October) until 8 October 2026 the trading pop-ups were not filed at all (the pop-up was no longer a direct child of the page), so the calculator, the chart and the wrapped there were never warmed.
- **First launch: everything is built before the opening plays** (`warmPrime`, `primeOne`, `gfxCold`, `PRIME_*`, `.open-prep` in `App.jsx`; `gfxHome`, `gfxCold`, `gfxPrimed`, `aiReady` in `electron/main.cjs`; 8 October 2026). He installed the DMG and reported that on its first opening the intro "just freezes" and everything lags, "all the ui in all the panels".
  - **The cause**: macOS keeps the graphics programs an app has built in a cache of that app's own (`$(getconf DARWIN_USER_CACHE_DIR)/<the helper's bundle id>/com.apple.metal`). To it, `npm run app` (`com.github.Electron.helper`) and the installed app (`com.rassam.flow.helper`) are two apps. So the cache is empty on the installed app's first launch, on any other Mac, and again after a macOS update. A program then takes 20 to 650ms to build instead of one, and the graphics process can show no frame meanwhile. Measured on an empty cache: the graphics process was stuck for 3.4 of the opening's 4.2 seconds (65ms with a full cache) and for 7.2s over the finance journey, with 24 frames more than a tenth of a second late.
  - **The cure**: when the shell finds that cache empty, the opening does not start yet. On the still backdrop, with one line ("Setting up for the first time") and a bar that is moved in steps (nothing that could stutter), every screen filed by the warm-up is drawn, and after them the shipped screens (`warm-seed.json`) he has none of his own for; the first panel, and the login of somebody new, are built underneath; the AI helper's own one-time setup runs meanwhile (it took 24s when measured, and used to start eight seconds into the app; the page waits for it, 30s at most). Then the opening plays as always.
  - How a screen is drawn there (`primeOne`), each step one frame, however long that frame takes: as the warm-up draws it; its animations held still at four points of their run (`.warm-screen.live`, `--at`: a line half drawn, a card half faded in); every second element repainted in place, then the other half; what has a layer of its own, every picture and whatever is blended, once a little see-through and once a little turned; every drawing once cut off at one side; and a long page to its end, each window-height the same way. Programs built after the opening on the finance journey: 179 with nothing, 50 with the screens drawn as the warm-up draws them, 4 with all of it.
  - **Measured afterwards on an empty cache** (the hidden engine, a copy of his entries): the preparation takes about 12s; then the graphics process is stuck 0.03s during the opening and 0.28s over the whole finance journey (was 7.2s), 2 frames more than a tenth of a second late (was 24), the longest 0.18s (was 1.45s). With an empty profile as well (somebody new, the shipped screens): 12 frames late over the login and all four sides, the longest 0.47s. On a cache that is already full the preparation takes 4 to 6s.
  - **When it happens**: the shell leaves a note in that same cache folder (`flow-primed`, naming the system and the engine version) once the page says it is done; no note, another system or engine, or the cache gone: the page is told (`--flow-gfx-cold` → `window.flowGfx.cold`). So: once per installed app per Mac, once for `npm run app`, and once after each macOS or Electron update. Every other launch is exactly as before; the timed warm-up under the opening is untouched. A change of account in the same session does not repeat it (`flow-primed` in `sessionStorage`). If the window is hidden meanwhile it gives up and tries again at the next launch; whatever happens, the app opens after 45s.
  - **The panels before an app are gone through as well** (`stageTour`, `warm.tour`, `TOUR_*`, `.th`; 11 October 2026: on the installed app, after the preparation, "all the 3d animations on the selections panel, trading and finance ones lag when first open"). The filed screens hold none of it: the coin and the die are left out of them, and what a press, the pointer or a toss draws is in no markup. Measured on an empty cache (journey `stage`): 22 programs were still built there, the graphics process standing still 110 to 350ms at seven of the first moves (the trading panel opening, the globe and the die coming into the middle, the roll, the toss, the next wallet, the card under the pointer).
    - After the screens the **real stage** is taken through all of it behind the backdrop, by presses and a pointer of the code's own making: wallet ⇄ coin, the trading panel, each of its three in the middle, under the pointer and on its way in, the wallets, the next wallet and back, a card out, under the pointer and on its way in. A way in is played and taken back: `enterTrade` and `enterApp` look at `warm.tour` and enter nothing. At the end the stage is back as it began.
    - **What made it work, each found by measuring; don't trim any of it**:
      - The covers (`.open-panel`, `.acc`) are 0.4% see-through meanwhile: what is wholly covered is not drawn.
      - What a press sets moving first **runs by itself** until it has run evenly for 120ms (a late frame gives the run its time back), and is only then held and stepped through to its end. Held from the start, 7 programs were left: something that runs by itself is drawn another way. What runs on keyframes (the toss) is stepped finely, more so at both ends: every strength of blur is a program.
      - **The pointer is a hand's**: it rests where it was put, leaves one thing only as it arrives on the next, and stays on what it pressed. Three programs were only built with a wallet's shine still fading while the next press moved it, or a thing leaning while on its way.
      - **A `:hover` on the stage has a `.th` beside it** (ten rules): no `:hover` answers to a pointer made by script, and the coin's light on the trading panel is one. **A new `:hover` on the stage needs its `.th`.**
      - **A second go** when more than five frames came late in the first (an empty cache: 14): what script moves a frame at a time has no time to give back, and its states behind a standstill were never drawn.
    - **Measured afterwards**, somebody new on an empty cache, through the login, the introduction, every one of those moves and into all four apps: **no program built at all**, no standstill of the graphics process over 26ms (was 350). The preparation takes longer for it: 33s in the hidden engine on an empty cache (13.5s before; the two goes 9.7s and 7.3s), 13.5s on a full one (one go, 7.8s). On his 120Hz display the stepped part goes at twice the pace.
    - `GFX_VER` in `electron/main.cjs` ends in a figure that is raised when the preparation draws more than it did (it is 3 since 20 October 2026): every installed app then goes through it once more.
    - **The sample screen** (`PRIME_KIT`, 20 October 2026: he installed the DMG and found scrolling down lagged "even after the setting up for the first time bar"). The filed screens alone left graphics programs to be built on first use. The preparation now draws one made-up screen first: fills in every shape the panels use (with `will-change`, in rounded clips), the app's pieces at full and half size, the day tiles and year cells in each state, scaled solid fills in clips, charts small, stretched and card-sized with 2, 4 and 8-stop gradients bare and clipped, and big gradient panes. While it files the screens it also plays their entrances (`.warm-screen.live`: the cards' `flowPopIn`, the month's `mvIn`, Home's `dgRise` and `wdSwap`), as those are drawn with programs of their own while they move.
    - Measured with the cold Electron copy (`coldscroll --cold --trace`): programs built after the opening went from 7 to 4, the longest standstills from 432 + 209ms to about 250 + 270ms. What is left is built the first time finance's month opens and is scrolled (`CoverBoundsRenderStep[NonAAFill]` with an 8-stop gradient, `TessellateWedgesRenderStep[Convex]` with a 4-stop gradient, MSAA): their source was not found in the samples. Once per install; every launch after that reuses them. The build before Home had the same kind (304ms on crypto's year scrolled).
    - Next step if he still feels it: halve the month's elements on a cold copy until the two programs stop appearing, then add that piece to `PRIME_KIT`.
  - **What it can't reach**: the greeting and the big year on the way into finance, which only exist once an app is entered. Measured on the same run: no program and no standstill there.
  - **Three more things, found the same day when he tried it and still saw the first openings lag** (he was running `npm run app`, where the cache is full, so it was not the programs):
    - **The rubbish of the preparation** (dozens of screens drawn and thrown away) was cleared by the engine a moment later, in the middle of the first presses: the page held up 42 and 25ms as the app was entered, 50 and 60ms on the first press of the coin. It is cleared at the end of the preparation, while nothing moves (`window.gc()`; the shell passes `--expose-gc` on such a launch only).
    - **His saved screens of the trading pop-ups were empty** (11 of 28: see the warm-up), and an empty one of his own kept the shipped one out. A filed screen under `WARM_STUB` characters counts as none, in the preparation and in the timed warm-up. The shipped screens he has none of his own for are also filed for him at the end (a day old, so his own go first), so later launches warm them too.
    - **The AI helper** stayed in memory for five idle minutes after its setup; on such a launch it is let go as soon as the setup is over.
  - **Measured after those**, in the hidden engine with a copy of his saved screens (full cache; `--cold`): nothing built and nothing stuck from the month on; a first opening keeps the page busy 20 to 60ms (a month, the calculator, a chart), a second 10 to 20ms. That rest is the script and the layout of the thing itself, not something left unprepared: in a profile the month's first and second opening spend the same time in the same functions, and it varies by a factor of two from run to run.
  - **Tried and of no use, don't retry**: compiling all the script up front (`--js-flags=--no-lazy`, with and without `--no-lazy-feedback-allocation`): over three runs each the first openings took 165, 157 and 198ms in all.
  - **Don't**: copy a cache in from another app's folder (tried: the system does not take it), or bring back `enable-skia-graphite-precompilation`. Don't make the line of text or its bar animate.
  - `warm-seed.json` is made of test data only, never of his entries (it ships in the app). To make it again after the look of the app has changed, each with `--profile=seed`: `seedprep --fresh` (an empty data folder: the login, then test data on all four sides), then `finance`, `cards`, `flow`, `trade`, `stocks`, `fx` (every screen is opened and files itself), `year-finance`, `year-crypto`, `year-idx`, `year-fx` (the yearly panels' pop-ups), then `dump`, and copy the output folder's `warm-seed.json` to `public/`. A stale set still does most of the work: the programs depend on the kinds of paint, not on the words.
  - The installed app has to be built again (`npm run dmg`) and put in Applications for any of this to reach it.
