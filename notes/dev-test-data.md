# Developer options and test data

One area of Flow. What applies everywhere, and the list of the other areas, is in the project's `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Developer options** (`devOpt`, `setDevOpt`, `useDevOpt`, `DEV_KEY`; 13 October 2026: "make it so i am able to enable and disable developer options, which is just the add/clear test data and everything developer mode wise"): the last row of Settings on every side, On / Off. Off, every test tool is gone at once: Add / Clear test data in the footers (and with them the test-data settings), Test levels in the calculators, Reset account in the sidebar.
  - It is the app's, not an account's: kept under `flow-dev` ("1" / "0") beside `flow-users`, nobody's data, in no backup, untouched by Reset account. With nothing saved it is what `SHOW_TEST_TOOLS` near the top of the file says (true): **it starts on, as the app was**; I did not make it start off for anybody. If the installer for the people he knows should start with it off, that is that one constant, but the `tools/perf` journeys on an empty profile press the flask and would need `flow-dev` set first.
  - **"Restore my entries" is not under it**: while something is put aside the row shows whatever the switch says. A new test tool reads `useDevOpt()` in its component (a hook, so at the top), never the constant.
  - Checked with the `others` journey: on to begin with (the flask, the sweep, Reset account), Off took all three away at once and saved "0", still off after the page was loaded again, On brought them back. Not pressed: Test levels with it off (the same value in the two calculators), the trading sides' footer (the same rows).
- **Test data, finance side: the developer options** (`TestCfg`, `testMonth`, `cleanTestCfg`; 5 October 2026). One click on the flask adds with the saved settings, a double click opens the pop-up. He asked to choose the days income arrives, when spending happens, a spending style that isn't confusing, "more variables", and for the bulk to sit in drop-downs.
  - Four sections that fold, each saying what it is set to in one line while shut: Income (amount, and a 1–31 calendar of the days it arrives; a month without the day skips it), When spending happens (days of the week, a 1–31 calendar, stop at today, no-spend marks), How much is spent (a preset only fills in the figures under it: small / medium / big buys, weekend, spike days, month swing, monthly bills; changing one makes it "Custom"), Range and extras (months, replace the old test data, sample goals, sample debts (on unless switched off, since 6 October 2026: two he owes, two owed to him; one set only, a second click adds no more), a seed: same seed, same data).
  - The generator is one pure function between two markers (`/* ---------- test data: the generator ----------`), which `tools/projection-test.mjs` reads out of the file for its backtest. The monthly bills have names of their own ("IndiHome", "PLN"): "Wifi" is read as "Internet" (`CANON`) and was mixed up with the random buy of that name.
  - The pop-up is a module-level component with its own draft; its switches use the draft as it is at that moment. Generated debts carry `test: true`, stay out of the export and go with "Clear test data".
